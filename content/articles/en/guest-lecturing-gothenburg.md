# Guest lecturing in Gothenburg: universities and business schools that invite practitioners

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

If you work in engineering, finance, business or law and want to teach a session in Gothenburg, this guide covers two institutions: Chalmers University of Technology and the University of Gothenburg, including its School of Business, Economics and Law. Both publish ways for practitioners to take part in teaching. They differ in who they address and who you should contact.

This guide sets out what each institution's own pages say, which course types and formats use outside practitioners, and how to approach the right person. We also give both institutions' published dates for 2026/2027, because timing often decides whether a course team can fit you in. Everything here comes from pages we opened on 16 November 2026. Where a page is silent, we say so.

For the wider list of Gothenburg speaking venues, including student societies, meetups and events, see [speaking opportunities in Gothenburg](/en/speaking-opportunities-gothenburg).

## Key takeaways

- Chalmers says it looks for guest lecturers for several of its courses. Alumni contact the Alumni Office. Organisations can contact the programme directors.
- Chalmers lists many ways to contribute beyond a lecture: seminars, workshops, project courses, case suggestions, study visits and thesis supervision.
- The University of Gothenburg's central alumni function invites alumni to guest lecture. Its School of Business, Economics and Law directs organisations to External Relations.
- The school's Centre for Finance runs professional seminars with invited speakers from outside academia. Its partner programme for companies includes the chance to guest lecture, in consultation with the responsible teacher.
- Chalmers splits the year into four study periods. Spring 2027 starts on 18 January at both Chalmers and the University of Gothenburg. Pitch before the period or term in which your course runs.
- None of the pages say whether guest lecturers are paid or what language sessions are in. Ask.

## Chalmers University of Technology

### What Chalmers says

Chalmers' alumni page is clear: "We are looking for guest lecturers to several of our courses, which always is appreciated by the students." It asks interested alumni to contact the Alumni Office at alumni@chalmers.se and describe their subject of expertise [[1]](#ref-1).

Chalmers also has a page for organisations, large or small, that want to take part in education. In Swedish it asks: "Vill du medverka i utbildningen? Det finns flera sätt att bidra!" ("Do you want to take part in education? There are several ways to contribute!") [[2]](#ref-2).

### Course types and formats open to practitioners

The same page lists ways to contribute within programmes and courses [[2]](#ref-2):

| Format | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Guest lecturer (vara gästföreläsare) | A session inside an existing course |
| Showing career paths (visa på karriärvägar) | A talk about your work and route into it |
| Seminars and workshops | An interactive session, often smaller groups |
| Project courses | Working with students on a real problem |
| Suggesting exercises or cases | Supplying a case from your industry |
| Study visits | Students visit your workplace |
| Supervising a student, thesis projects | Longer commitments |
| Alumni events, mentoring, programme councils | Ongoing relationships with a programme |

Chalmers also describes its Tracks courses as including "ett nära samarbete med externa aktörer och företag", close collaboration with external actors and companies, who are welcome to take part and contribute [[2]](#ref-2).

If a full lecture feels like too much for a first step, a case suggestion or a seminar may be an easier way in. It also gives the course team a reason to remember you.

### Who to contact at Chalmers

- **Alumni:** the Alumni Office, alumni@chalmers.se, with your subject of expertise [[1]](#ref-1).
- **Organisations and other practitioners:** the programme directors. Chalmers' collaboration page says to contact them about working together on education [[2]](#ref-2). Each programme has a management team of a programme director, an education secretary and a study counsellor, listed through the [programme contacts page](https://www.chalmers.se/utbildning/studentstod/studievagledning-och-stod/kontaktpersoner-per-program/) [[3]](#ref-3).
- **Reaching students outside courses:** the same page says the student union is often the contact if you want to reach Chalmers students [[2]](#ref-2).

A programme director sees the whole programme, so they can point you to the course where your topic fits. That is useful if you are not sure which course to target. Our guide on [finding the right course for a guest lecture](/en/find-the-right-course-for-a-guest-lecture) explains how to read course pages.

## University of Gothenburg

### Central alumni function

The University of Gothenburg's alumni page lists guest lecturing at the university, mentoring and inspiring students in their career choices as ways to get involved. It also mentions taking part in alumni meetings, lectures, webinars and network meetings. Contact is alumn@gu.se [[4]](#ref-4).

The page frames this as an alumni offer. If you did not study at the university, approach a course or department directly.

### School of Business, Economics and Law

The School of Business, Economics and Law (Handelshögskolan) calls itself a meeting place for students, researchers, alumni, business and society. It says collaboration also happens within research and education. Organisations interested in collaborating are asked to contact External Relations at handelshogskolan@handels.gu.se, or the centres working in specific areas [[5]](#ref-5).

For alumni, the school's alumni page invites them to share knowledge and experience with future generations of students. Through Career Service, alumni can also help with thesis topics, internships and recruitment [[6]](#ref-6).

The school's public event programme mostly consists of named lectures and seminars, and a Kunskapslunch series where researchers present their work [[7]](#ref-7). These are not presented as open slots for practitioners, so treat them as a way to learn what the school cares about rather than as a pitch target.

### Centre for Finance

The Centre for Finance at the school runs professional seminars for the business community and finance sector, with invited speakers from outside academia. Past examples include talks by speakers from IFN, Greenhill & Co and Kommuninvest. It also organises conferences where practitioners and academics meet, some held with AP2 and Handelsbanken [[8]](#ref-8).

This suits finance practitioners whose topic has a research angle. The page does not describe how speakers are invited, so the centre's own contacts or External Relations are the routes to try.

### Partner programme

The school's partner programme started in 1999 and has three levels: Senior Partner, Associate Partner and Career Partner. Among the benefits, the page lists the possibility "att gästföreläsa på kurser och program i samråd med ansvarig lärare": to guest lecture on courses and programmes in consultation with the responsible teacher. Career Service also helps partners arrange events and meetings with students. Partners can join reference groups, roundtables and advisory boards. The page names a business liaison and a working-life coordinator as contacts [[9]](#ref-9).

This route is for organisations, not individuals. If your employer is already a partner, it may be the quickest way into a course, because the school has already agreed to it. The page lists current partners, so check it before you ask internally. It does not say what a partnership costs.

## How the academic year runs in Gothenburg

A course team plans its sessions before the course starts. If you write when the course is already running, the schedule is usually full. So it helps to know the calendar.

### Chalmers: four study periods

Chalmers publishes these study periods (läsperioder) and exam periods for 2026/2027 [[10]](#ref-10):

| Study period | Dates | Exam period |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 31 August to 1 November 2026 | 24 to 30 October 2026 |
| 2 | 2 November 2026 to 17 January 2027 | 9 to 16 January 2027 |
| 3 | 18 January to 21 March 2027 | 13 to 20 March 2027 |
| 4 | 22 March to 6 June 2027 | 29 May to 5 June 2027 |

Each period ends with an exam period, so teaching sessions fall in the weeks before it. Check the course page or syllabus to see which study period your target course runs in. For example, if your target course runs in study period 4, starting 22 March 2027, aim to reach the programme director during study period 3 or earlier.

### University of Gothenburg: terms

The University of Gothenburg publishes these term dates [[11]](#ref-11):

| Term | Dates |
|---|---|
| Spring 2027 | 18 January to 6 June 2027 |
| Autumn 2027 | 30 August 2027 to 16 January 2028 |

The university notes that some programmes start and end on other dates, and refers to its course and programme catalogue for each programme's dates [[11]](#ref-11). Check the course page to see when in the term your target course runs. Our guide to the [academic calendar for guest lectures](/en/academic-calendar-guest-lectures) explains how to time a pitch across Swedish institutions.

## How to approach a Gothenburg course

1. **Choose your route.** Alumni use the alumni route. Everyone else writes to a programme director (Chalmers) or External Relations (Handelshögskolan).
2. **Match a format.** Decide whether you offer a lecture, a seminar, a case or a study visit. Chalmers lists all of these.
3. **Name the course or programme.** A specific fit is easier to accept. See [how to become a guest lecturer at a university](/en/become-a-guest-lecturer-at-a-university).
4. **Time it.** Contact the programme before the study period or term in which the course runs. Use the dates above as your deadline.
5. **Follow up once.** One polite follow-up is enough. Our [guest lecture email templates](/en/guest-lecture-email-templates) include one.

## What we could not confirm

- **Payment.** No page states whether guest lecturers are paid or get expenses.
- **Language.** No page we checked states the teaching language for guest sessions.
- **Autumn 2026 at the University of Gothenburg.** The university page we used lists spring 2027 and autumn 2027 only. Individual programmes can also differ.
- **When a specific course runs.** The calendar pages give periods and terms, not course schedules. Check the course page.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I guest lecture at Chalmers if I am not an alumnus?
Chalmers' page on collaborating in education is aimed at organisations of any size and lists guest lecturing as one way to contribute. It points you to the programme directors.

### Who do I contact at the University of Gothenburg's School of Business, Economics and Law?
The school asks organisations interested in collaborating to contact External Relations at handelshogskolan@handels.gu.se. Alumni can also use the university's central alumni function at alumn@gu.se.

### What if I cannot commit to a full lecture?
Chalmers lists smaller and different contributions: suggesting a case or exercise, running a seminar or workshop, or hosting a study visit. These can be a good first step.

### When should I contact a Gothenburg course about a guest lecture?
Before the study period or term in which the course runs. At Chalmers, study period 3 starts on 18 January 2027 and study period 4 on 22 March 2027. The University of Gothenburg's spring term 2027 runs from 18 January to 6 June.

### Are guest lecturers paid in Gothenburg?
The pages we checked do not say. Ask the programme or course team before you agree.
