# Speaking opportunities in Malmö and Lund: universities, student societies and networks

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

If you work in southern Sweden and want to speak more, Malmö and Lund are best treated as one market. Many of the groups that host talks draw people from both cities, and some meetups move between them.

The hard part is not a lack of audiences. It is knowing who decides. At a university it is usually the teacher responsible for a course. At a student society it is a committee that changes every year. At a network or meetup it is a small organising team.

This page lists real organisations in Malmö and Lund that host external speakers or describe ways for professionals to contribute. Each entry links to the organisation's own page, and we checked every one on 16 November 2026. We do not promise that any of them has a slot for you. We describe where opportunities typically come from, so you can decide where to pitch.

## Key takeaways

- Lund University's Faculty of Engineering (LTH) says its courses welcome guest lecturers, but you first need a relationship with the teacher responsible for the course [[1]](#ref-1).
- At LUSEM, guest lectures are framed around partner companies and run through Career Services [[2]](#ref-2).
- Student organisations such as LINC and LundaEkonomerna run guest lectures and lunch lectures, often linked to corporate relations [[7]](#ref-7) [[10]](#ref-10).
- Innovation hubs and networks (AI Lund, Ideon, Media Evolution, Minc, the Southern Sweden Chamber of Commerce) host seminars where outside experts present [[14]](#ref-14) [[15]](#ref-15) [[16]](#ref-16).
- Tech meetups are the most open door: .NET Skåne has a call for speakers open until 1 October 2027 [[22]](#ref-22).

## Why Malmö and Lund work as one speaking market

Lund University and Malmö University are separate institutions, but their student, startup and tech communities overlap. A few concrete signs from the pages we checked:

- .NET Skåne says its meetups are typically held in the Malmö, Lund or Helsingborg region [[22]](#ref-22).
- Lund University runs teaching in Malmö too. For example, its Music Management & Business course is taught at the Malmö Academy of Music [[27]](#ref-27).
- Ideon Science Park in Lund listed a seminar hosted at Swedbank in Malmö among its upcoming events [[15]](#ref-15).

In practice this means one well-prepared talk can be offered to several audiences within a short trip. For the general method, read our guide on [how to get speaking opportunities](/en/how-to-get-speaking-opportunities).

## How to read this list

- Every organisation below was checked on its own official page on 16 November 2026.
- Entries are grouped by type and ordered alphabetically within each group. This is not a ranking.
- "What the page says" summarises what the organisation publishes. Where a page does not mention guest speakers, we say so.

## Universities and schools

This section gives a short overview. For course types, timing and how to reach a course coordinator, see our guide to [guest lecturing in Malmö and Lund](/en/guest-lecturing-malmo-lund).

### Lund University: Faculty of Engineering (LTH)

**What the page says:** LTH's courses "welcome guest lecturers and/or arrange study visits to companies and organisations". The page adds that a company "first needs to establish a relationship with the teacher responsible for the course". An External Engagement Coordinator, named on the page, can help you find the right programme, course and course coordinator [[1]](#ref-1).

**What this means for you:** The decision sits with the course teacher. The central contact helps you find that person. Our guide to [becoming a guest lecturer at a university](/en/become-a-guest-lecturer-at-a-university) explains how to approach a course coordinator.

[LTH: Guest lecture and study visit](https://lth.se/english/collaborate/collaborate-with-our-students/guest-lecture-and-study-visit) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Lund University School of Economics and Management (LUSEM)

**What the page says:** "Partner companies can make a meaningful impact by sharing their expertise through guest lectures at LUSEM." The same page describes company presentations and roundtable discussions, and says Career Services can help you find a suitable path [[2]](#ref-2). A wider collaboration page also lists case work (judging, live cases), thesis projects and hubs for start-ups and sustainability [[3]](#ref-3).

**What this means for you:** The formal route is built around partner companies. As an individual, you can still contact a course director directly. LUSEM itself reported a guest lecture in which a course director invited two designers whose book was the course's main literature [[4]](#ref-4).

[LUSEM: Business meets students](https://www.lusem.lu.se/collaborate/collaborate-our-students/business-meets-students) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Malmö University

**What the page says:** The "Collaboration with students" page lists degree projects, project assignments, internships, career events and the Stormathon innovation competition. It does not mention guest lectures [[5]](#ref-5). Separately, MaU Innovation tells lecturers it can offer "suggestions for inspirational speakers to enrich various teaching modules" [[6]](#ref-6). Some course pages describe outside visitors. A master's course in the School of Arts and Communication (K3) says "Students will meet local and/or international creative and cultural actors" [[8]](#ref-8).

**What this means for you:** There is no single guest-lecture form. Look for courses close to your field and contact the course coordinator, or make yourself known to MaU Innovation if your topic is innovation or entrepreneurship.

[Malmö University: Collaboration with students](https://mau.se/en/collaboration-and-innovation/collaboration-with-students/) (checked 16 November 2026)

## Student unions, societies and nations

Student organisations plan their programmes term by term and change boards often. Our guide on [speaking at student societies](/en/speak-at-student-societies) covers timing and tone.

### LINC: Lund University Finance Society

**What the page says:** LUSEM describes LINC as offering "inspiring guest lectures, practical analysis groups, workshops, social events, mentoring programmes" [[7]](#ref-7). LINC's own site says it was founded in 1991, has more than 3,700 members and lists a business-enquiries address [[9]](#ref-9).

[LINC: About](https://linclund.com/about/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### LundaEkonomerna (student union at LUSEM)

**What the page says:** LundaEkonomerna is the student union for LUSEM students and part of Studentlund. Its committees include a Corporate Relations Committee (CRC). Its site lists a past CRC Breakfast Club, described as an interactive lecture on startups and entrepreneurship [[10]](#ref-10). A 2023 article in the union's magazine describes CRC lunch lectures where a company representative spoke about AI, digitalisation and auditing [[11]](#ref-11).

[LundaEkonomerna](https://www.lundaekonomerna.se/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Studentlund, student unions and nations in Lund

**What the page says:** Lund University explains that Studentlund brings together the nations, Akademiska Föreningen and the student unions. Each faculty has its own union, and the unions run career days, lectures and welcome activities. There are 13 student nations, mainly focused on social life [[12]](#ref-12).

**What this means for you:** Faculty unions and their committees are the more likely hosts for a professional talk. Nations are mainly social.

[Lund University: Studentliv](https://www.lu.se/studera/livet-som-student/studentliv) (checked 16 November 2026)

### UF Malmö (Utrikespolitiska Föreningen Malmö)

**What the page says:** A student-run, independent non-profit whose activities include "Lectures, debates, pub quizzes, podcasts, and trips". The page does not say whether lectures use guest speakers [[13]](#ref-13).

[UF Malmö](https://ufmalmo.se) (checked 16 November 2026)

### UPF Lund (The Association of Foreign Affairs in Lund)

**What the page says:** "Every month throughout the academic year, UPF invites guests to lecture on a diverse range of topics." The Lecture Committee has its own address on the page [[18]](#ref-18).

[UPF Lund: Lectures](https://upflund.se/lecture/) (checked 16 November 2026)

## Innovation hubs, networks and associations

### AI Lund

**What the page says:** An open network for research, education and innovation in AI at Lund University. Under ways for industry to take part, it lists: "Share your real world experience as a guest speaker." [[14]](#ref-14)

[AI Lund: Collaborate](https://www.ai.lu.se/about/collaborate) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Ideon Science Park (Lund)

**What the page says:** Ideon describes itself as one of Sweden's leading science parks and incubators. Its events list on the check date included "The Tech Hub Breakfast – HØIBERG IP Strategy in the AI Age" on 19 November [[15]](#ref-15).

[Ideon Science Park](https://ideon.se/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Media Evolution (Malmö)

**What the page says:** A Malmö community with a programme of classes, seminars and social gatherings. Upcoming items on the check date included a seminar on 25 November 2026 [[16]](#ref-16). A June 2026 seminar gave each of its speakers a 10-minute slot; one came from Malmö University [[17]](#ref-17).

[Media Evolution](https://www.mediaevolution.se/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Minc (Malmö)

**What the page says:** Minc calls itself "A startup house of Malmö". Its event listings include a lunch seminar where Grant Thornton experts covered incentive programmes and R&D tax incentives [[19]](#ref-19).

[Minc event: Seminar with Grant Thornton](https://luma.com/thphi1qb) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Southern Sweden Chamber of Commerce (Sydsvenska Industri- och Handelskammaren)

**What the page says:** The chamber's work includes "hosting seminars" [[20]](#ref-20). It runs member networks such as Ekonomen (for finance departments, 6 to 8 meetings a year with talks) and a network for executive assistants whose members "lyssnar på föredrag" (listen to talks) [[21]](#ref-21). Its events page showed no events on the check date.

[Handelskammaren: Nätverk](https://handelskammaren.com/natverk/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### VentureLab (Lund University)

**What the page says:** Lund University's student entrepreneurship organisation. Its events page lists past workshops and talks, including a guest talk by a startup co-founder and a lunch talk by a founder and author. Several listings on the page are from 2023 and 2024 [[23]](#ref-23).

[VentureLab: Events](https://venturelab.lu.se/events-1) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Incubators and startup hubs: where the talks are

Two of the entries above are incubators or startup hubs, and both run recurring formats that use speakers.

- **Ideon Science Park** holds the Tech Hub Breakfast every Thursday at 09:00, a weekly gathering where people share knowledge and ideas. Ideon says it is looking for speakers. It also runs a Leadership Forum, a Branding, Marketing & Communications Forum and an HR network, and says most of its events are free of charge [[28]](#ref-28). [Ideon: community and networks](https://ideon.se/community-networks/)
- **Minc** runs "Meet Minc" breakfasts where people interested in the startup scene meet entrepreneurs and the Minc team [[29]](#ref-29), as well as expert seminars like the Grant Thornton session above [[19]](#ref-19). [Minc: Meet Minc](https://luma.com/8q7g27dz)

Checked 17 November 2026. We could not verify an events page for LU Innovation, so it is not listed. For a weekly slot like the Tech Hub Breakfast, a short, practical talk on one problem founders face is a better pitch than a general introduction to your field.

For other cities, see [speaking opportunities in Uppsala](/en/speaking-opportunities-uppsala) and our overview of [speaking opportunities in Sweden, city by city](/en/speaking-opportunities-sweden-by-city).

## Meetups and conferences

### .NET Skåne

**What the page says:** A community meetup with a call for speakers open from 1 November 2026 to 1 October 2027. Talks of 20 or 45 minutes, in English or Swedish, on anything useful to a .NET developer, including AI, cloud and real project experience [[22]](#ref-22).

[.NET Skåne: Call for Speakers](https://sessionize.com/net-skane/) (checked 16 November 2026)

### Øredev (Malmö)

**What the page says:** A developer conference held 4 to 6 November 2026 at MalmöMässan. Tracks included Applied AI & Data and Leadership. The call for papers closed on 31 March 2026. Travel and accommodation were covered for speakers [[24]](#ref-24).

**What this means for you:** If the pattern repeats, the call opens many months before the event. Watch the organiser's page in early spring.

[Øredev 2026: Call for Papers](https://sessionize.com/oredev-2026/) (checked 16 November 2026)

## How to approach organisers in Malmö and Lund

1. **Pick the audience first.** A finance talk fits LINC or LUSEM. An AI case study fits AI Lund or a meetup. A creative industry talk fits Media Evolution or a K3 course.
2. **Find the person who decides.** For a course, that is the course coordinator (kursansvarig). For a society, it is usually the committee that runs events.
3. **Write one short, specific pitch.** Name the course or event, the topic, the length and what students or members will take away.
4. **Never sell.** Meetups such as AWS User Group Stockholm state outright that sales pitches are not accepted, and most local organisers expect the same [[25]](#ref-25).
5. **Follow up once, then stop.** If there is no reply after one polite reminder, move on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I give a guest lecture at Lund University if I am not an alumnus?
Yes, that can happen. LTH's guest-lecture page is written for companies and organisations, not only alumni, and says you need a relationship with the course's responsible teacher [[1]](#ref-1). Lund University's alumni pages also invite alumni involvement more generally [[26]](#ref-26).

### Does Malmö University have a guest lecturer programme?
We did not find one. Its collaboration page lists degree projects, internships and other formats, but not guest lectures [[5]](#ref-5). Course coordinators and MaU Innovation are the more realistic routes [[6]](#ref-6).

### Are talks in Malmö and Lund given in English?
It depends on the organiser. .NET Skåne accepts English or Swedish [[22]](#ref-22). University course pages state the language of instruction; Lund University's Music Management & Business course, for example, is taught in Swedish [[27]](#ref-27). Check before you pitch.

### Are these speaking slots paid?
Most of the pages we checked say nothing about fees. Some conferences, such as Øredev, covered speakers' travel and accommodation [[24]](#ref-24). Assume unpaid unless the organiser says otherwise.
