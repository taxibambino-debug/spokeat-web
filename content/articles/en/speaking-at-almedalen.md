# Speaking at Almedalen Week: how to get onto a seminar

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

Almedalen Week (Almedalsveckan) in Visby is Sweden's largest meeting place for politics and public debate. For a professional who wants to be heard on a policy question in their field, a seat on an Almedalen panel is one of the most visible slots in the Swedish calendar.

But individuals rarely "apply to speak" at Almedalen. Seminars are arranged by organisations, and the organisers choose their own panels. So there are two ways onto a stage: help your organisation arrange a seminar, or get invited onto someone else's.

This guide explains how the week works, what the official rules and fees for 2027 are, and how to plan either route. Everything below comes from Region Gotland's official pages, checked on 17 November 2026.

## Key takeaways
- Almedalen Week 2027 runs from Monday 28 June to Friday 2 July in Visby [[1]](#ref-1).
- Seminars are arranged by organisations. Panellists are invited by those organisers, not selected by Region Gotland.
- Registering an event in the official programme is free until 17 March 2027. After that the fee rises in steps, up to 5,000 SEK per event [[2]](#ref-2).
- Official programme events must deal with a current societal issue, be free and open to everyone, and must not sell or launch a product [[3]](#ref-3).
- Most of the work happens in spring. If you want an invitation, talk to organisers in your field months before June.

## How Almedalen Week works

Almedalen Week began in 1968 with a speech by Olof Palme in Almedalen park in Visby. The parliamentary parties are the main organisers, through their local representatives on Gotland. Region Gotland is the formal host and runs the week through its project office [[4]](#ref-4).

Each parliamentary party has its own half day, with a party leader speech. Around those speeches, organisations of every kind hold seminars, debates and receptions. Organisations and businesses have been able to take part since 1996, and anyone can apply to arrange an event if they meet the criteria [[4]](#ref-4).

The scale is large. Region Gotland's final statistics for 2026 counted 3,170 events from 1,804 unique arrangers and 6,627 participants. Companies were the largest group of arrangers, with 889 events. The busiest days were Tuesday and Wednesday [[5]](#ref-5).

One detail matters for international professionals: 88% of 2026 events were held only in Swedish [[5]](#ref-5). English-language events exist, and the official programme can be filtered by language [[4]](#ref-4), but expect most panels to be in Swedish.

## Who arranges seminars, and what the rules are

Only events that meet Region Gotland's criteria are published in the official programme. The framework, in force since 1 January 2022, says an official event must [[3]](#ref-3):

- deal with a current societal issue
- be free of charge and open to everyone
- take place at a physical location in or near Visby's inner city
- follow Swedish law

Events that sell or launch a product or service, or only showcase an organisation's operations, are excluded [[3]](#ref-3). This is the most important rule for a speaker. A panel invitation at Almedalen is an invitation to discuss a societal question, not to present your company.

To register, an organisation needs one responsible organiser who accepts the terms. The event needs a title, a short description of the issue and a day and time. Speakers can be added later. The title may be at most 100 characters and should state the issue clearly. Names of people or organisations on the panel should not appear in the title or the issue description [[2]](#ref-2).

## Route 1: arrange a seminar through your organisation

If your employer, association or network wants to be at Almedalen, you can propose a seminar and sit on it. Region Gotland's checklist for organisers lists ten steps, from deciding how to take part and reading the framework to booking a venue, registering the event, arranging permits and planning security [[6]](#ref-6).

### What it costs to register in 2027

The fee is per event and depends on when you register [[2]](#ref-2) [[7]](#ref-7):

| Registration date (2027) | Fee per event |
|---|---|
| 14 January to 17 March | Free |
| 18 March to 14 April | 500 SEK |
| 15 April to 19 May | 1,000 SEK |
| 20 May to 9 June | 3,000 SEK |
| 10 June to 2 July | 5,000 SEK |

The last day to delete a published event in 2027 is 8 June. After that it is marked as cancelled instead [[7]](#ref-7).

### Other costs to plan for

The registration fee is usually the smallest cost. Region Gotland lists further charges, with 2026 rates where 2027 rates had not yet been set when we checked [[7]](#ref-7):

- **Outdoor ground space (markplats):** 154 SEK per square metre per day for central locations and 124 SEK for other locations (2026 rates, indexed).
- **Police permit applications:** 360 SEK for a public assembly, 990 SEK for a public event and 990 SEK for use of public space (2026 fees; check the Police Authority for current fees).

Venue hire, travel and accommodation come on top. Region Gotland advises organisers to book travel and accommodation well in advance [[8]](#ref-8). The ground-space map for 2027 opened for preliminary bookings in October 2026 [[9]](#ref-9).

### If you represent a non-profit that is new to Almedalen

Region Gotland runs "Alla Röster Ska Höras" (All Voices Should Be Heard). It gives non-profit civil society organisations that are new to the official programme, or have not taken part in the past four years, one hour on a stage with basic technology at no cost. For 2027, applications open in early December and close in early March, with answers in week 11 [[10]](#ref-10).

## Route 2: get invited onto someone else's panel

Most professionals who speak at Almedalen do it this way. An organiser has a societal issue and needs a panel that covers several perspectives. Your job is to be one of those perspectives, and to be known to the organiser before they fill the seats.

Large organisations co-organise many events and invite outside participants. Stockholm University's 2026 events, for example, included politicians, agency heads, rectors from other universities and representatives of unions and civil society [[11]](#ref-11).

A practical approach:

1. **Study last year's programme.** Search the official programme for your topic and note which organisations held events on it [[4]](#ref-4). Those are your likeliest hosts.
2. **Start with organisations you already belong to.** Trade associations, professional bodies and chambers of commerce often arrange Almedalen seminars. Our guide to [speaking at industry associations and chambers](/en/speak-at-industry-associations-and-chambers) explains how to build those relationships.
3. **Offer a perspective, not a presentation.** Say in two sentences what view you would bring to their issue and why it differs from others on a typical panel.
4. **Contact them early.** Free registration closes on 17 March 2027 [[2]](#ref-2), so many organisers plan panels in winter. Writing in May is late.
5. **Prepare for a panel format.** Almedalen slots are short and shared. Our guide on [how to be a good panellist and moderator](/en/panellist-and-panel-moderator-guide) covers preparation and on-stage habits.

## Is this "paying to speak"?

Not in the usual sense. The registration fee is paid by the arranging organisation for an event that must be free and open to the public. Still, arranging a seminar costs money and time, and a panel invitation does not usually come with a fee or travel paid. Read our guide to [paying to speak](/en/paying-to-speak) before you commit budget, and decide what visibility is worth to you. If you are based in the capital, our overview of [speaking opportunities in Stockholm](/en/speaking-opportunities-stockholm) lists year-round alternatives.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an individual register a seminar at Almedalen?
Registration is done through an organisation, which needs a responsible organiser who accepts the terms [[2]](#ref-2). Region Gotland says anyone can apply to arrange an event if they meet the criteria [[4]](#ref-4), but in practice most speakers take part through an organisation.

### When is Almedalen Week 2027?
Monday 28 June to Friday 2 July 2027, in Visby (week 26) [[1]](#ref-1).

### How much does it cost to register an event?
Registration in the official programme is free from 14 January to 17 March 2027. After that it costs 500, 1,000, 3,000 or 5,000 SEK per event, depending on the date [[7]](#ref-7). Venue, permits, travel and accommodation are extra.

### Can I promote my company in an Almedalen seminar?
No. Official programme events must not sell or launch a product or service, or only showcase an operation [[3]](#ref-3). Focus on the societal issue.

### Are Almedalen seminars in English?
Some are, but in 2026, 88% of events were held only in Swedish [[5]](#ref-5).
