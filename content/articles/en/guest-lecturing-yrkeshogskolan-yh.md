# Guest lecturing at Swedish higher vocational education (YH)

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

When professionals in Sweden think about guest lecturing, they usually think of universities. Many overlook higher vocational education, known in Swedish as yrkeshögskolan or YH. That is a missed opportunity, because YH programmes are built around working life, and their rules require close cooperation with employers.

YH students are training for a specific job. A guest lecture from someone who does that job today is directly useful to them. Programme managers also need industry contacts for workplace placements and for their management groups.

This guide explains how YH works, what the official rules say about industry involvement, who to contact, and how a guest lecture can lead to a longer relationship. It is written for international professionals working in Sweden. Check each programme's language of instruction before you offer a session in English.

## Key takeaways
- YH programmes are post-secondary vocational programmes, usually one to two years long, approved and supervised by the Swedish National Agency for Higher Vocational Education (Myndigheten för yrkeshögskolan, MYH) [[1]](#ref-1).
- The ordinance on YH says programmes should be delivered largely in cooperation with the parts of working life they serve [[2]](#ref-2).
- MYH lists guest lectures, study visits, mentoring and cases as ways for working life to take part in the school-based courses [[3]](#ref-3).
- The person to contact is usually the programme manager (utbildningsledare) at the school that runs the programme.
- Every programme has a management group (ledningsgrupp) in which working life holds the majority [[2]](#ref-2). A guest lecture can be a first step towards a seat.

## How YH works

YH is post-secondary education offered across Sweden in many fields. Programmes are usually one to two years long and lead to a degree and a clear occupational role. Courses are shorter, often around seven weeks. Most programmes are free of charge and qualify for student finance from CSN [[1]](#ref-1).

MYH approves and supervises programmes, but it does not run them. Schools, known as education providers (utbildningsanordnare), deliver the teaching, and students apply directly to each school [[1]](#ref-1). Providers can be municipalities. Luleå municipality, for example, runs YH programmes through its adult education service [[4]](#ref-4).

YH is large. According to Statistics Sweden (SCB), 34,500 people were admitted to and started a YH programme in 2025, and there were about 104,600 applicants for 33,400 places. Engineering and manufacturing had the most new students [[5]](#ref-5). MYH says about 87,000 people study in a YH programme each year [[6]](#ref-6).

## Why YH programmes need people from industry

Industry involvement is not a nice extra in YH. It is written into the rules.

The Ordinance on Higher Vocational Education (förordning 2009:130 om yrkeshögskolan) says that programmes should be carried out largely in cooperation with the parts of working life concerned [[2]](#ref-2). It also requires workplace learning, known as LIA (lärande i arbete). For programmes leading to the advanced vocational degree, at least a quarter of the study time must be LIA at a workplace. A new provision in force from 1 April 2027 sets the same minimum for programmes in general [[2]](#ref-2).

MYH's quality criteria say the management group should ensure a strong connection to working life in every part of the programme. They also say teaching staff should know the occupation the programme prepares students for [[7]](#ref-7).

MYH itself names guest lectures as one way in. At a YH day for working-life representatives in Gothenburg on 12 March 2026, MYH listed "management group visits, guest lectures, study visits, mentoring, cases" as ways for working life to take part in the school-based courses [[3]](#ref-3).

## The management group: where working life decides

Every YH programme is governed by a management group (ledningsgrupp). The ordinance says the majority of its members must represent working life, with some exceptions [[2]](#ref-2). The group also includes at least one representative of the school and at least one student representative. It admits applicants, decides on credit transfer and is responsible for the programme's quality work [[2]](#ref-2).

MYH says about 10,000 working-life representatives currently take part in management groups [[6]](#ref-6). That is a large network of employers who already shape YH teaching. If you know someone who sits on one in your field, ask them how the programme uses guest speakers.

## Who to contact

In YH, the key person is usually the programme manager, often called utbildningsledare. Luleå municipality's quality report, for example, states that its programmes are organised and coordinated by designated programme managers, who also report to the management group [[4]](#ref-4).

In Luleå's report, the programme manager keeps in regular contact with students and presents follow-up results to the management group [[4]](#ref-4). That makes the programme manager the right first person to ask whether a guest lecture fits a course. Teachers who run a specific course may also decide, so ask the programme manager who is responsible for the course you have in mind.

Our article on [how to become a guest lecturer at a university](/en/become-a-guest-lecturer-at-a-university) explains the same principle for universities: decisions are made close to the course, not centrally.

## How to find YH programmes in your field

MYH directs people to yrkeshogskolan.se to find programmes and contact details for the schools that run them [[1]](#ref-1) [[6]](#ref-6). MYH also lists regional YH networks on its website [[6]](#ref-6).

Read each programme description and its course list. You are looking for a course where your daily work is part of the learning outcomes. Our guide on [how to find the right course for a guest lecture](/en/find-the-right-course-for-a-guest-lecture) shows how to read course descriptions, and the method works for YH too.

## How to offer a guest lecture

1. **Pick one course.** Name it in your email. A specific offer is easier to accept than "I can talk about my industry".
2. **Link your topic to the job.** YH students are training for a defined role. Show how your session helps them do that job.
3. **Offer formats.** A short talk, a case from your workplace, a study visit or a Q&A. These match the forms of involvement MYH lists [[3]](#ref-3).
4. **Mention placements if you can help.** Providers must make sure there are LIA places [[2]](#ref-2). If your employer could host a student, say so.
5. **Be clear about language.** If you can only teach in English, say so up front and ask whether that works for the students.

Our [guest lecture email templates](/en/guest-lecture-email-templates) give you a starting structure.

## Timing

YH programmes run on their own schedules, and courses can be short. A seven-week course leaves little room to add a guest once it has started [[1]](#ref-1). Ask the programme manager when the relevant course runs and when it is planned, and write before the planning is fixed. Our guide to [the academic calendar for guest lecturers](/en/academic-calendar-guest-lectures) covers planning cycles at universities, and the same logic of contacting teachers before a course starts applies here.

## Next step: joining a management group

A guest lecture is a good way to show what you know. If the relationship works, you may be asked to join the programme's management group. Working-life members help set requirements and keep learning outcomes in line with what employers need [[3]](#ref-3) [[6]](#ref-6). It is not a speaking role, but it gives you regular contact with the programme and its students.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do YH programmes use guest lecturers?
MYH lists guest lectures among the ways working life can take part in YH courses, along with study visits, mentoring and cases [[3]](#ref-3). Whether a given programme uses them depends on the course and the programme manager.

### Who decides whether I can give a guest lecture at a YH school?
Usually the programme manager (utbildningsledare) or the teacher responsible for the course. Programme managers organise and coordinate the programmes [[4]](#ref-4).

### Is YH the same as a university?
No. YH is post-secondary vocational education, usually one to two years, run by education providers and supervised by MYH [[1]](#ref-1). Universities are governed by different rules.

### Do I need to speak Swedish?
Not always, but check the programme's language of instruction. If you can only teach in English, ask the programme manager whether that would work for their students.

### What is a ledningsgrupp?
It is the management group that governs each YH programme. Most of its members must represent working life [[2]](#ref-2).
