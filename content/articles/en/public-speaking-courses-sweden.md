# Public speaking and presentation courses in Sweden: the directory

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

You want to get better at speaking in front of people. Maybe you freeze in meetings, maybe you have a big presentation coming up, or maybe you simply want more practice. Sweden has plenty of options, but they are spread across universities, study associations, volunteer clubs and private trainers, and each one describes itself differently.

This directory pulls them into one place. Every provider listed here has a page we opened on 16 November 2026. For each one we note the type, format, level, length, language and price, but only when the provider publishes a price on its own page.

This page is the national overview. The detailed listings sit on separate pages for [Stockholm](/en/public-speaking-courses-stockholm), [Gothenburg](/en/public-speaking-courses-gothenburg), [Malmö and Lund](/en/public-speaking-courses-malmo-lund), [online courses](/en/online-public-speaking-courses) and [university rhetoric courses](/en/university-rhetoric-courses).

## Key takeaways
- There are four main routes, plus self-paced online platforms: university courses (credit-bearing, applied for through the national admissions system), study associations (short evening courses), clubs such as Toastmasters (low-cost, ongoing practice) and private trainers (intensive, usually employer-paid).
- University rhetoric courses run from short half-time courses to full 30-credit terms. Most are taught in Swedish.
- Study association courses are short and cheap. Medborgarskolan lists four-session evening courses in Stockholm at 2,250 SEK [[12]](#ref-12).
- Toastmasters clubs meet in Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö, Lund and online, and several meet in English.
- Private providers rarely publish prices. NobleProg is an exception for some courses, and it has venues in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö [[20]](#ref-20).
- Not sure which type suits you? Read our [guide to choosing a public speaking course](/en/choose-a-public-speaking-course).

## The types of course at a glance

| Type | Typical format | Typical length | Language | Cost (where published) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| University course | Campus or distance, part-time or full-time | 3 to 30 credits, one term or part of a term | Mostly Swedish | No fee for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens at Lund University [[8]](#ref-8); LiU lists 13,500 SEK for students from outside the EU/EEA [[9]](#ref-9) |
| Study association | Evening sessions, small group | 1 to 6 sessions | Mostly Swedish | 299 to 5,975 SEK in the listings we checked [[11]](#ref-11) [[12]](#ref-12) |
| Club (Toastmasters and similar) | Regular meetings, in person, hybrid or online | Ongoing | English, Swedish or both | Several clubs say guest visits are free; membership fees not published on the pages we checked |
| Private provider | Intensive workshops, online live courses or corporate training | 1 to 3 days | Swedish or English | Rarely published; NobleProg lists some courses in euros [[20]](#ref-20) |
| International online platform | Self-paced video course | A few hours to 8 weeks | English | Free options exist; certificates usually cost extra [[26]](#ref-26) [[27]](#ref-27) |

## Universities and university colleges

University courses give you structure, a teacher, graded assessment and academic credits (högskolepoäng). You apply through the national admissions round, usually by 15 April for autumn and 15 October for spring. Several courses below show these dates on their own pages [[1]](#ref-1).

Courses we verified:

- **University of Gothenburg** runs *Retorik, grundkurs* (30 credits, full-time, campus, Swedish), which also covers nerves, voice training and memory technique [[1]](#ref-1). It also runs *Retorik för yrkesverksamma* (7.5 credits, half-time, afternoons), aimed at people who speak for a living [[2]](#ref-2).
- **Stockholm University** lists *Praktisk retorik* (15 credits, daytime), which applies classical rhetoric to professional communication [[3]](#ref-3). The course is given within a bachelor's programme in education, and its page shows no separate application round [[24]](#ref-24).
- **Södertörn University** in Flemingsberg offers standalone courses including *Retorik A*, *Retorik i praktiken* and *Våga tala: praktiska övningar*, plus a rhetoric bachelor's programme [[4]](#ref-4). The *Våga tala* course is half-time, on campus, and the university notes that about half the group comes from working life [[5]](#ref-5).
- **Uppsala University** runs *Retorik A* (30 credits, full-time, daytime, campus, Swedish), with the next start on 18 January 2027 [[23]](#ref-23), and a three-year rhetoric programme (180 credits) in Swedish [[6]](#ref-6). Its rhetoric department also sells tailored contract education to employers [[7]](#ref-7).
- **Lund University** runs *Retorik: Grundkurs* (30 credits, full-time, campus, Swedish) [[8]](#ref-8).
- **Linköping University** runs *Retorik: Att tala, distanskurs* (7.5 credits, quarter-time, location-independent) [[9]](#ref-9) and *Retorik: Att leda och lyssna i yrkeslivet* (7.5 credits, quarter-time, distance) [[10]](#ref-10).

Full details, eligibility and terms are on our page of [university rhetoric courses](/en/university-rhetoric-courses).

## Study associations (studieförbund)

Study associations run short courses for adults, usually in the evening, at a fraction of the cost of private training. What is on offer changes every term, so check the provider's own course search.

What we found on 16 November 2026:

- **Folkuniversitetet** listed *Retorik i arbetslivet* in Stockholm (10 sessions from 10 March 2027, 5,975 SEK) and *Våga tala: bli bekväm med att prata inför andra* in Gothenburg (3 sessions from 1 December 2026, 1,125 SEK) [[11]](#ref-11). In Malmö it runs *Public Speaking in English* (see [Malmö and Lund](/en/public-speaking-courses-malmo-lund)).
- **Medborgarskolan** listed four rhetoric courses and workshops in Stockholm starting in January 2027 (299 to 2,250 SEK). In Skåne it listed *Våga tala! Presentationsteknik del 1* in Ystad (4 sessions from 26 November 2026, 1,395 SEK) and *Retorik: Konsten att nå fram* in Kristianstad (6 sessions from 18 February 2027, 1,599 SEK) [[12]](#ref-12). It also listed free "try it" sessions with the Luleå Toastmasters club, held in English [[12]](#ref-12).
- **Studiefrämjandet** describes rhetoric as a study circle topic and invites you to start your own circle, but its rhetoric page showed no scheduled course [[13]](#ref-13).
- **Studieförbundet Vuxenskolan (SV)** and **ABF** both name rhetoric in their reading, writing and speaking category, but neither listing showed a speaking course in Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö or Lund when we checked [[14]](#ref-14) [[15]](#ref-15).

## Clubs: Toastmasters and others

Toastmasters International calls itself "a nonprofit educational organization". It reports over 270,000 members in more than 14,000 clubs in 150 countries [[16]](#ref-16). At a meeting, members give prepared speeches, answer impromptu questions and give each other feedback [[16]](#ref-16).

Clubs suit you if you want regular stage time rather than a one-off course. In Sweden we verified open clubs in:

- **Stockholm:** several clubs, including English-speaking and bilingual ones. See [public speaking courses in Stockholm](/en/public-speaking-courses-stockholm).
- **Gothenburg:** five clubs open to the public, several of them hybrid. See [public speaking courses in Gothenburg](/en/public-speaking-courses-gothenburg).
- **Malmö and Lund:** Lund Toastmasters meets on Wednesdays (odd weeks) at Ideon in Lund [[18]](#ref-18), and Malmö Toastmasters meets every other Monday at Slagthuset in Malmö [[25]](#ref-25). See [Malmö and Lund](/en/public-speaking-courses-malmo-lund).
- **Online:** Svenska Talarklubben Online meets on Zoom at lunchtime on Thursdays (odd weeks) [[17]](#ref-17). See [online public speaking courses](/en/online-public-speaking-courses).

Some company clubs are closed to outsiders. We list only clubs whose Toastmasters page says membership is open to all.

## Private providers

Private trainers and training companies run intensive courses, often for employers. Few publish prices. We list only providers whose own page we could open.

- **Dale Carnegie Sweden** has a head office in Stockholm and regional contacts for Gothenburg, Malmö and the north of Sweden [[19]](#ref-19). Its Swedish site does not publish public course dates or prices for presentation training, so contact the regional office.
- **Göteborgs Tekniska College** runs an 8-hour company course, *Speech: Effective communication*, in Gothenburg for groups of 8 to 12 [[22]](#ref-22).
- **NobleProg** runs presentation courses of 7 to 21 hours, online live or at its venues in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö. It lists prices for some courses, for example 3,000 EUR online for the 21-hour Presenting with Confidence, but the page does not say whether that is per participant or per group [[20]](#ref-20) [[21]](#ref-21).

We found other private providers only through course marketplace sites. We could not open their own websites, so we left them out. If you run one, see the correction note below.

## International online platforms

If you want to study theory at your own pace, platforms such as Coursera, edX and FutureLearn host public speaking courses in English from universities. Examples we checked: the University of Washington's *Introduction to Public Speaking* on Coursera (free to enrol, certificate paid) [[26]](#ref-26), HarvardX's *Rhetoric: The Art of Persuasive Writing and Public Speaking* on edX (8 weeks; free to audit, certificate 209 USD) [[27]](#ref-27) and the Open University's *Talk the Talk* on FutureLearn (6 weeks, described as free) [[28]](#ref-28). Details are on our page of [online public speaking courses](/en/online-public-speaking-courses).

## How we compiled this directory

- **Independent.** No provider paid to be listed, and no listing is sponsored. We do not earn commission from any course.
- **Criteria.** A listing must (1) teach speaking, presentation or rhetoric, (2) be open to the public or to anyone who meets standard university eligibility, and (3) have its own page that we could open. We left out courses only open to students already on a specific programme, company clubs with restricted membership, and providers we could find only on third-party sites.
- **Facts.** Format, level, length and language come from the provider's page. We show a price only where the provider publishes one, with the date we saw it.
- **Order.** Listings are ordered by type and then alphabetically. They are not ranked.
- **Last checked:** 16 November 2026. Dates, prices and availability change every term, so confirm with the provider before you book.

**Providers:** if a detail is wrong or you want your course considered, you can request a correction. See [how to request a correction](/about/editorial-policy).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a public speaking course cost in Sweden?
It depends on the type. In the listings we checked, study association courses ranged from 299 SEK for a single workshop to 5,975 SEK for a longer course. University courses carry no tuition fee for EU/EEA and Swiss citizens at Lund University, while LiU lists 13,500 SEK for students from outside the EU/EEA. Several Toastmasters clubs say guest visits are free.

### Can I take a public speaking course in English in Sweden?
Yes, mainly through clubs and short courses. Stockholm International Toastmasters and Gothenburg Toastmasters Club meet in English, and Stockholm Speakers Club is bilingual. Folkuniversitetet in Malmö runs Public Speaking in English, and Lund, Linköping and Malmö universities run some courses in English. Most university rhetoric courses we found are taught in Swedish.

### Do I need university eligibility for a university rhetoric course?
Most introductory courses only require basic eligibility (grundläggande behörighet). Some, such as Retorik för yrkesverksamma at the University of Gothenburg, require earlier university credits, and advanced-level courses require a degree.

### Is Toastmasters a course?
Not in the usual sense. It is a nonprofit network of clubs where members practise speeches and give each other feedback at regular meetings. It works well alongside a course, or after one, if you want ongoing practice.

### How often is this directory updated?
We last checked every listing on 16 November 2026. Course dates and prices change each term, so always confirm on the provider's own page.
