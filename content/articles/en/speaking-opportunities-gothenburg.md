# Speaking opportunities in Gothenburg: universities, student societies and networks

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

Gothenburg is home to the University of Gothenburg and Chalmers, student unions with their own societies, and tech groups that meet around AI and digital identity. Several of these openly invite outside speakers. Finding out which ones, and through which door, takes time.

This page lists only organisations whose own pages show that they host speakers or invite proposals. We opened every page on 16 November 2026. For each, we say what the page shows, the contact route it publishes and what it leaves out. We do not rank them. Within each section they appear in alphabetical order.

If you are new to speaking, start with our guide on [how to get speaking opportunities](/en/how-to-get-speaking-opportunities).

## Key takeaways

- Chalmers says it looks for guest lecturers for several courses, and asks alumni to contact its Alumni Office. Its collaboration page lists guest lecturing as a way for organisations to contribute.
- The University of Gothenburg's central alumni function invites alumni to guest lecture. Its School of Business, Economics and Law runs professional seminars at its Centre for Finance with speakers from outside academia.
- To reach Chalmers students, Chalmers itself says the student union is often the right contact. Chalmers Börssällskap's most common activity is lunch lectures by people from business, economics and politics. At the University of Gothenburg, the IT student association behind the GÖSTA career fair runs breakfast and lunch lectures with companies.
- In tech, GAIA, AWS User Group Gothenburg, Cloud Native Göteborg and ReactJS Göteborg hold evening meetups with talks. Data Saturday Gothenburg and KuppingerCole's Nordics Identity Impact Day 2027 take talk proposals through public forms.
- Only KuppingerCole states what speakers receive (a free conference registration). No page states a speaker fee.

## How to use this list

Each entry links to the organisation's own page and gives the route it publishes. We have not listed anyone's personal email.

Before you write, check three things:

1. **Who the route is for.** University alumni routes are for alumni. Other routes are open to organisations.
2. **Timing.** Courses are planned before term starts. Our [academic calendar guide](/en/academic-calendar-guest-lectures) explains when to pitch.
3. **Language.** Few Gothenburg pages we checked state the language of talks. Ask if it matters to you.

## Universities and schools

### Chalmers University of Technology

The [Chalmers alumni page](https://www.chalmers.se/en/about-chalmers/alumni/get-involved/) says: "We are looking for guest lecturers to several of our courses, which always is appreciated by the students." Alumni who are interested should contact the Alumni Office at alumni@chalmers.se and describe their subject of expertise [[1]](#ref-1).

Chalmers' [page on collaborating in education](https://www.chalmers.se/samarbeta-med-oss/samarbete-i-utbildning-och-med-studenter/) is aimed at organisations of any size. It lists being a guest lecturer, showing career paths, running seminars or workshops, joining project courses and suggesting cases. For questions about collaborating on education, it points to the programme directors [[2]](#ref-2). Each programme has a management team with a programme director, an education secretary and a study counsellor, listed on Chalmers' [programme contacts page](https://www.chalmers.se/utbildning/studentstod/studievagledning-och-stod/kontaktpersoner-per-program/) [[3]](#ref-3).

- **Fits:** engineering, technology, industry practice, project and case work.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### University of Gothenburg: central alumni function

The [University of Gothenburg alumni page](https://www.gu.se/studera-vid-goteborgs-universitet/efter-studierna/alumn-vid-goteborgs-universitet) (in Swedish) lists ways alumni can take part: guest lecture at the university, be a mentor or inspire students in their career choices. Contact is alumn@gu.se [[4]](#ref-4).

- **Fits:** alumni from any faculty.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### University of Gothenburg: School of Business, Economics and Law

The School of Business, Economics and Law (Handelshögskolan) describes itself as a meeting place for students, researchers, alumni, business and society. Its [collaboration page](https://www.gu.se/handelshogskolan/om-oss/samverkan) asks organisations interested in working together to contact External Relations at handelshogskolan@handels.gu.se [[5]](#ref-5). Its [alumni page](https://www.gu.se/handelshogskolan/om-oss/samverkan/valkommen-som-alumn) invites alumni to share their knowledge and experience with future generations of students [[6]](#ref-6).

The school's [Centre for Finance](https://www.gu.se/en/centre-for-finance) runs professional seminars for the business community and finance sector, with invited speakers from outside academia. Past examples include speakers from IFN, Greenhill & Co and Kommuninvest. It also runs conferences where practitioners and academics meet [[7]](#ref-7).

If your employer is a larger company, check the school's [partner programme](https://www.gu.se/handelshogskolan/om-oss/samverkan/bli-partner-till-handelshogskolan). It started in 1999 and has three levels. Among the benefits it lists is the chance to guest lecture on courses and programmes, in consultation with the responsible teacher. Career Service also helps partners arrange events where they meet students. The page names a business liaison and a working-life coordinator as contacts [[8]](#ref-8).

- **Fits:** business, economics, finance, law.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The Centre for Finance page does not describe how speakers are invited. The partner programme is an agreement with an organisation, not an individual, and the page does not say what it costs.

For how these institutions use guest lecturers in courses, see [guest lecturing in Gothenburg](/en/guest-lecturing-gothenburg). For the general process, read [how to become a guest lecturer at a university](/en/become-a-guest-lecturer-at-a-university).

## Student unions and societies

Student societies book speakers for lunch lectures and company events. Our guide to [speaking at student societies](/en/speak-at-student-societies) explains how their boards work.

### Chalmers Börssällskap (CBS)

Chalmers Börssällskap is listed by the Chalmers student union as the economic association that promotes economics and entrepreneurship. The listing says its most common activity is lunch lectures by well-known people from business, economics and politics [[9]](#ref-9). Its own site shows a [lunch lecture with the CEO of SKF](https://cbs.chalmers.se/events/rickard-gustafson-ceo-skf/) on 29 April 2025, covering his career, leading a large industrial company and SKF's planned split, followed by Q&A [[10]](#ref-10).

CBS also runs evening formats. On 2 December 2025 it held a [case evening on cryptocurrencies](https://cbs.chalmers.se/?p=4846) with a vice dean from the School of Business, Economics and Law: a lecture of about 25 minutes, a case and discussion of about 45 minutes, then dinner and mingling [[11]](#ref-11). If a full lunch lecture does not suit your topic, a short talk plus a case is a format this society already uses.

- **Fits:** business leaders, finance, entrepreneurship.
- **Route:** the contact page on the CBS site.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### Chalmers Kristna Grupp (CKG)

The Chalmers student union's [list of societies and committees](https://chs.chalmers.se/list-of-societies-and-committees) says CKG arranges "quite popular lunch lectures every year" [[9]](#ref-9).

- **Checked 16 November 2026.** We did not open CKG's own site. Start from the union's list.

### Chalmers Studentkår (Chalmers student union)

Chalmers' own collaboration page says that if you want to reach Chalmers students, it is often the student union you should contact [[2]](#ref-2). The union's [page for companies](https://www.chs.chalmers.se/how-our-companies-work) describes Chalmers Studentkår Promotion, an event and marketing company owned by the union, which connects students with businesses. The general desk is desk@chalmersstudentkar.se [[12]](#ref-12). The union's society list also describes the CHARM career fair, where over 170 companies come to meet students [[9]](#ref-9). CHARM is a fair, not a lecture series, but it shows which companies already invest in reaching Chalmers students.

- **Fits:** organisations that want to reach engineering students.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The page does not describe lecture slots specifically.

### GÖSTA Student Association (University of Gothenburg)

GÖSTA is a student association for IT students at the University of Gothenburg. The university's [event page](https://studentportal.gu.se/en/event/gosta-career-fair-for-it-students-2026) for its 2026 career fair says around 30 companies and organisations took part on 19 February 2026 at Lindholmen Conference Centre. Alongside the fair there was a breakfast lecture and a lunch lecture with companies, free but with a separate ticket [[13]](#ref-13).

- **Fits:** software, IT and data, especially if your company recruits IT graduates.
- **Route:** the event page points to the association's website and its Instagram account.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** We did not open the association's own website.

### University of Gothenburg student unions

The University of Gothenburg's [student portal](https://studentportal.gu.se/en/student-life/student-unions-and-associations) lists five student unions: Göta studentkår, HHGS (School of Business, Economics and Law), SAKS (Sahlgrenska Academy), Konstkåren (Faculty of Fine, Applied and Performing Arts) and the doctoral union GUDK. It also lists the cooperation bodies GUS, GFS and SFS [[14]](#ref-14). This is the place to find the right union for your field, since associations sit under their own union.

- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The portal lists the unions but does not describe their events.

### World Values Initiative (Chalmers)

The Chalmers student union's [list of societies and committees](https://chs.chalmers.se/list-of-societies-and-committees) says the World Values Initiative runs events with inspirational speakers and workshops [[9]](#ref-9).

- **Checked 16 November 2026.** We found no separate page with a contact route; start from the union's list.

## Tech meetups and events

Meetups are often the easiest first slot. See our guide to [getting a first meetup talk](/en/meetup-talks-first-slot).

### AWS User Group Gothenburg

The [AWS User Group Gothenburg](https://www.meetup.com/aws_user_group_gothenburg/events/306788202) held an evening on 10 April 2025 with two talks on developer experience on AWS, hosted at Volvo Group Connected Solutions. The event page invites anyone with an AWS idea or story to get in touch with the organisers "and we'll discuss to make it happen" [[15]](#ref-15).

- **Fits:** cloud, DevOps and software engineering on AWS.
- **Route:** message the organisers through Meetup.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The page we opened is a past event; check the group for upcoming dates.

### Cloud Native Göteborg (CNCF community group)

[Cloud Native Göteborg](https://ocgroups.dev/cncf/group/goteborg/event/9zqwcj5) is a local group in the Cloud Native Computing Foundation's community. Its 20 May 2026 meetup on AI was hosted by Kognic at Lindholmen, with two talks, and was sold out [[16]](#ref-16).

- **Fits:** Kubernetes, platform engineering, infrastructure, applied AI in engineering teams.
- **Route:** no call for speakers on the page; use the "Contact organizers" button.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### Data Saturday Gothenburg

[Data Saturday Gothenburg](https://sessionize.com/data-saturday-gothenburg-2026/) is a free one-day community conference on the Microsoft data platform and Azure. The 2026 edition, on 5 September 2026, was described as the 11th the organisers have run in Gothenburg. Its call for speakers ran from 23 March to 30 April 2026 and is now closed. Topics included data management, cloud and hybrid architecture, analytics, business intelligence and AI [[17]](#ref-17).

- **Fits:** data engineers, BI and analytics specialists, data platform architects.
- **Route:** the Sessionize call for speakers. The 2025 call closed on 1 April 2025, so if the pattern holds, watch for the 2027 call in early spring.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The page does not say whether speakers get travel or other support.

### GAIA: Gothenburg Artificial Intelligence Alliance

[GAIA](https://www.meetup.com/gaia-gbg) is a non-profit association promoting AI, machine learning, data science and data engineering in Gothenburg. It hosts meetups with talks and workshops from academia and industry, and a yearly GAIA Conference. Recent meetups were held in September and October, and the 2026 conference took place at Svenska Mässan in April. The group has about 1,756 members [[18]](#ref-18).

- **Route:** there is no formal call for speakers. The page invites people to come and share their machine learning experience, so message the organisers through Meetup.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### Göteborg AI (Solita Sweden's meetups)

[Göteborg AI](https://www.meetup.com/goteborg-new-technology-meetup-group) discusses AI from technical and societal angles, including AI in product development, organisational change, ethics, regulation and the future of work. It has about 546 members. Its most recent event was held in person at Solita in Gothenburg in October [[19]](#ref-19).

- **Route:** no call for speakers; message the organiser through Meetup.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** This group is run by a company, Solita.

### Nordics Identity Impact Day 2027 (KuppingerCole Analysts)

[Nordics Identity Impact Day 2027](https://www.kuppingercole.com/events/niid2027) takes place on 9 February 2027 at the Riverton Hotel in Gothenburg, for around 100 participants in identity and access management [[20]](#ref-20). Its [call for speakers](https://www.kuppingercole.com/events/niid2027/callforspeakers) has a "Propose a Talk" form. Suggested topics include AI, identity and trust, zero trust, and digital identity wallets. Speakers receive a complimentary conference registration. No submission deadline is published [[21]](#ref-21).

- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### ReactJS Göteborg

[ReactJS Göteborg](https://www.meetup.com/reactjs-goteborg/) covers anything in the React and React Native ecosystem, from design systems to state management and architecture. It has about 1,473 members and lists 36 past events. Recent evenings were hosted by local companies, including eTraveli Group in October 2025 and, more recently, Devies and Kvadrat Göteborg [[22]](#ref-22).

- **Fits:** frontend and mobile developers.
- **Route:** no call for talks; message the organisers through Meetup. No upcoming event was listed.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.**

### Invest in Gothenburg tech calendar

This is not an organiser but a useful finding tool. The [tech calendar](https://www.investingothenburg.com/key-sectors/tech/tech-calendar?page=0) on Invest in Gothenburg is published by Business Region Göteborg, the city's economic development agency. It lists tech conferences, seminars, workshops and network meetings in the region, and organisations can submit their own events. In late November 2026 it listed, for example, a TechMeetUp organised by Gothenburg Tech and a Gothenburg Technical Leaders Community evening for CTOs and technical leaders [[23]](#ref-23).

- **Checked 16 November 2026.** We did not open those organisers' own pages, so check them before pitching.

## Business networks

Business networks book speakers for breakfasts, seminars and member evenings. Our guide to [speaking at industry associations and chambers](/en/speak-at-industry-associations-and-chambers) covers how to approach them.

### Företagarna Göteborg

[Företagarna Göteborg](https://www.foretagarna.se/foreningar/goteborg/) is the Gothenburg branch of Företagarna, the Swedish association for business owners. Its page lists member events such as a go-kart evening with Företagarna Young (18 November), a seat-swapping dinner at Liseberg (30 November) and a Tjejvasan event with Volvo and Företagarna W (26 February 2027). It also mentions a seminar on the Serneke court cases that it organised for members with experts. The branch contact is gbg@foretagarna.se [[24]](#ref-24).

- **Fits:** topics for small and medium-sized business owners, such as law, finance, HR and growth.
- **Checked 16 November 2026.** The page does not describe how speakers are chosen.

## Incubators and startup hubs

Incubators can bring in outside experts to teach founders about funding, law, tax and hiring, as the example below shows. Such sessions are usually for the companies they support, not the public, so the way in is to offer specialist knowledge their founders need.

### Chalmers Ventures

[Chalmers Ventures](https://chalmersventures.com/?p=22569) runs a "Venture Platform" of workshops and events for its portfolio startups. The page lists, for example, a master class on raising venture capital, a workshop on incentive programmes with corporate lawyers and tax experts, a session on the legal side of hiring and a seminar with a law firm on foreign direct investment rules. The page appears to date from autumn 2023 [[30]](#ref-30). Its Venture Day in May 2025 at Chalmers included panel discussions, investor meetings and showcases from the Chalmers Ventures portfolio [[31]](#ref-31).

- **Fits:** venture funding, law, tax, incentive programmes, hiring, deep tech.
- **Checked 17 November 2026.** The sessions are for portfolio companies, and the page does not describe how experts are chosen. Experts in the examples above came from law firms, accounting firms, investors and a government agency.

We could not verify current event pages for other Gothenburg incubators, so we have not listed them.

For other cities, see [speaking opportunities in Uppsala](/en/speaking-opportunities-uppsala) and our overview of [speaking opportunities in Sweden, city by city](/en/speaking-opportunities-sweden-by-city).

## Worth checking, with limits

These organisations host talks, but we could not open their own websites on 16 November 2026. We list what other official pages show, so you can check further.

- **Handelshögskolans studentkår (HHGS),** the student union at the School of Business, Economics and Law. The school's own [student union page](https://www.gu.se/en/school-business-economics-law/student-union) says HHGS is the parent organisation of more than 30 associations, whose events range "from inspirational lunch lectures to a full-blown musical", and that it runs three career fairs a year [[25]](#ref-25). A University of Gothenburg article (published 2021, updated 2024) says its association HaSS organises lectures on sustainability and workshops, and that the union runs the GADDEN career fair [[26]](#ref-26). The university's student portal links to the union's website [[14]](#ref-14).
- **Västsvenska Handelskammaren (West Sweden Chamber of Commerce).** A University of Gothenburg page describes it as a politically neutral membership organisation for the region's businesses, founded in 1661, with about 2,900 member companies [[27]](#ref-27). Its press room shows its 2023 Chamber Day, with seminars and presentations from regional companies [[28]](#ref-28). In October 2025 it hosted a leadership breakfast lecture at its premises, organised with the WIN WIN Gothenburg Sustainability Award [[29]](#ref-29).

## What we could not confirm

- **Payment.** No page states a speaker fee. KuppingerCole gives a free registration. Ask before you accept.
- **Talk language.** Most pages do not say whether talks are in English or Swedish.
- **Schedules.** Most student societies and meetups listed here did not show upcoming dates on the pages we checked. Data Saturday's next call for speakers is not yet published.
- **Own websites.** We could not open the websites of HHGS, the West Sweden Chamber of Commerce, CHARM or GÖSTA during this check.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to be an alumnus to guest lecture in Gothenburg?
The University of Gothenburg and Chalmers alumni routes are for alumni. Chalmers' collaboration page also lists guest lecturing as a way for organisations to contribute, and points to the programme directors.

### How do I reach Chalmers students with a talk?
Chalmers' own page says the student union is often the contact for reaching students. Societies such as Chalmers Börssällskap host lunch lectures with business leaders.

### Are there open calls for speakers in Gothenburg?
Yes, though few. KuppingerCole's Nordics Identity Impact Day 2027 takes proposals through a form. Data Saturday Gothenburg ran its calls on Sessionize in spring in both 2025 and 2026; the 2026 call closed on 30 April. The Invest in Gothenburg tech calendar is a good place to spot other events.

### Which tech meetups in Gothenburg accept speakers?
AWS User Group Gothenburg invites people with an AWS story to contact the organisers. GAIA, Cloud Native Göteborg and ReactJS Göteborg hold evenings with talks but publish no formal call, so message the organisers with a short talk idea.

### Will I be paid?
None of the pages we checked state a speaker fee. KuppingerCole offers speakers a complimentary registration. Ask each organiser.
