# Speaking opportunities for HR and leadership experts

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

HR leaders and experienced managers deal every day with what management courses describe in theory: hiring, conflict, reorganisations, union negotiations, sick leave and change. Students on HR programmes know they will face these situations soon, and they want to hear from people who already have.

Leadership talks are also among the most crowded topics on the conference circuit. That makes the less visible venues more valuable: HR programmes at universities, student HR associations, peer networks and research centres that bring practitioners and researchers together.

This page shows where HR and leadership experts speak, with real examples we checked on 17 November 2026. We do not promise that any organiser has a slot for you. We describe where opportunities come from and what each type expects.

## Key takeaways

- HR programmes in Sweden build in contact with working life. Umeå University's HR programme calls guest lectures "an important part" of its teaching [[2]](#ref-2).
- Management courses invite experienced managers. KTH's Management course has had managers from Scania and AstraZeneca take part in reflection sessions [[1]](#ref-1).
- Student HR associations run their own events, such as a careers day with company workshops and guest lectures [[5]](#ref-5).
- HR networks run many small meetings. Sveriges HR-förening reported 40 active networks and five regional boards in 2025 [[6]](#ref-6).
- Large HR technology conferences often reserve speaking slots for practitioners and route vendors to sponsorship [[11]](#ref-11).

## University courses and HR programmes

Swedish HR programmes (personalvetarprogrammet, also called personal- och arbetslivsprogrammet) train future HR specialists. Several describe their links to working life in detail, which tells you how a guest might fit in.

**Examples we verified:**

- **Umeå University's HR programme.** A programme document from 2021 states that "an important part of the teaching consists of guest lectures", with past topics such as rehabilitation and union negotiations. Several practising HR specialists are part of the teaching team, and third-year students do about ten weeks of work placement [[2]](#ref-2).
- **Stockholm University's PAO programme** (personnel, work and organisation) is run by three departments together. The programme works with the HR sector through an advisory board and lists the student association Stockholms Universitets Personalvetarförening (SthlmUP) as a contact point [[3]](#ref-3).
- **University of Gävle's HR programme** links student groups with organisations. The organisation appoints a contact person, and students carry out assignments such as interviews and organisational analyses [[4]](#ref-4). This is not a guest lecture, but it is a way to get to know a programme.
- **KTH**, course HU1903 Management (7.5 credits). The course memo for spring 2025 says experienced managers from Scania and AstraZeneca would take part in reflection sessions and share their experience [[1]](#ref-1).

**Course types to look for:** human resource management, organisational behaviour, leadership, work psychology, labour law, change management and industrial engineering courses with a management module. Our guide on [finding the right course](/en/find-the-right-course-for-a-guest-lecture) explains how to read a syllabus for signs that guests are welcome.

## Student HR associations

HR students often have their own association, separate from the wider business or social science student union. These groups run careers days and company events.

**Examples we verified:**

- **Pelikan at Karlstad University.** In 2023, a project group from the student association ran Personalvetardagen, a careers day for HR students with company workshops, guest lectures and networking. Many participating companies focused on HR and recruitment [[5]](#ref-5).
- **SthlmUP (Stockholms Universitets Personalvetarförening)**, named by Stockholm University as a link between the HR programme and its students [[3]](#ref-3).

**What they usually want:** honest stories about the job, practical skills (for example how you run an interview), and time for questions. Student associations change committees every year, so check who is in charge before you write.

## HR associations, networks and research centres

HR people learn a lot from peers. That makes networks a good place for a practical talk with a narrow topic. Our guide on [speaking at industry associations and chambers](/en/speak-at-industry-associations-and-chambers) explains how member programmes are put together.

**Examples we verified:**

- **Sveriges HR-förening** (the Swedish HR Association). Its annual report for 2025 lists five regional boards, 40 active networks and eight webinars. Its conference HR-dagarna took place on 22 and 23 October 2025 with over 400 participants [[6]](#ref-6).
- **TechSverige's People in Tech network**, for people working with HR in the tech industry. It is free but only for TechSverige members, meets regularly in five locations, and "occasionally" features guest speakers who share their experience [[8]](#ref-8).
- **Centre for Global HRM (CGHRM), University of Gothenburg**, which works with partner organisations through regular partner meetings, advisory work and seminars, and runs open seminars and lectures, usually as webinars [[9]](#ref-9). Its VåHR-festen on 29 April 2026 combined doctoral research presentations with a panel of practitioners, including the SVP HR of Volvo Group and the HR director of Norrköping Municipality [[10]](#ref-10).

## Conferences and podcasts

HR conferences range from member events to large international shows with commercial speaking packages. Read the rules before you apply.

- **HR Technology Conference Europe 2027** takes place in Amsterdam on 21 and 22 April 2027. Its call for proposals closed on 11 September 2026. Submissions were open to HR and HRIT practitioners, analysts, consultants, authors and research organisations, while solution providers were told to use sponsorship packages instead [[11]](#ref-11).
- **Rethink! HR Tech Nordic 2027** is listed for 27 and 28 September 2027 in Copenhagen and describes itself as bringing together "over 200 CHROs and senior HR decision-makers" [[12]](#ref-12). Its ticket page gave no speaker application details when we checked.
- **Fråga forskaren** ("Ask the researcher") is Sveriges HR-förening's official podcast. In April 2026 it interviewed two researchers about AI at work, its first episode in English [[7]](#ref-7). The association reported 19 episodes in 2025 [[6]](#ref-6). The format features researchers, so it suits HR experts who also do research.

For conferences with open calls, read our [call for speakers guide](/en/call-for-speakers-guide).

## Topics that tend to work

These are illustrative examples, not guaranteed formats:

- What a reorganisation looked like from the HR side, without confidential details.
- How you prepare for and run a negotiation with a union, as in Umeå's guest lecture topics [[2]](#ref-2).
- What a first year as a manager actually involves, for students and new leaders.
- How a company introduced AI tools in recruitment or HR administration, and what it learned.
- How employer branding and HR work together. Our article on [guest lectures and employer branding](/en/guest-lectures-employer-branding) covers the recruitment side.

Experts who also work with finance, tech or communications can find more ideas in our pages on [finance and fintech](/en/speaking-opportunities-finance-fintech), [tech and AI](/en/speaking-opportunities-tech-ai) and [marketing and communications](/en/speaking-opportunities-marketing).

## How to pitch as an HR or leadership expert

1. **Be specific.** "Leadership" is crowded. "What we changed after three failed manager onboardings" is a talk.
2. **Protect people.** Never use identifiable cases about employees. Students and peers want the lesson, not the gossip.
3. **Show credibility briefly.** Your role, the size of the organisation and one concrete result.
4. **Keep recruitment separate.** If you also want to recruit, say so openly and let the organiser decide whether it fits.
5. **Follow up once.** If there is no reply, move on to the next organiser.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can an HR manager give a guest lecture?
Start with HR programmes (personalvetarprogrammet) at Swedish universities. Umeå's programme describes guest lectures as an important part of teaching [[2]](#ref-2), and Stockholm's PAO programme works with the HR sector through an advisory board [[3]](#ref-3).

### Do HR conferences accept proposals from consultants?
Some do. HR Technology Conference Europe 2027 accepted proposals from consultants but told solution providers to use sponsorship packages [[11]](#ref-11). Always read the eligibility rules.

### Is an HR network a good place for a first talk?
Often, yes. Sveriges HR-förening reported 40 active networks in 2025 [[6]](#ref-6), and TechSverige's People in Tech network occasionally invites guest speakers [[8]](#ref-8). Small groups make a good first audience.

### How do I reach HR students?
Contact the HR student association at the university, such as SthlmUP in Stockholm or Pelikan in Karlstad [[3]](#ref-3) [[5]](#ref-5), and ask about careers days and guest lectures.
