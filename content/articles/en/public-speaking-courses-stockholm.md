# Public speaking courses in Stockholm

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

Stockholm has more ways to practise public speaking than any other Swedish city. You can study rhetoric for a full term at university, take a four-evening course at a study association, or drop in on a Toastmasters club for free. The hard part is comparing them, because each provider describes its courses differently.

This page lists the options we could verify on 16 November 2026. Every listing links to the provider's own page. We show prices only where the provider publishes them. Listings are grouped by type and ordered alphabetically, not ranked.

For courses elsewhere in Sweden, see the [national directory of public speaking courses](/en/public-speaking-courses-sweden).

## Key takeaways
- **Cheapest way to start:** three Toastmasters clubs meet at Folkuniversitetet on Kungstensgatan 45, and two of them say guest visits are free.
- **Short evening course:** Medborgarskolan lists four-evening courses from late January 2027 at 2,250 SEK [[7]](#ref-7).
- **Credits and depth:** Södertörn University runs open credit-bearing rhetoric courses, from half-time to full-time. Stockholm University's *Praktisk retorik* is given within a bachelor's programme.
- **In English:** your best options are Stockholm International Toastmasters, the bilingual Stockholm Speakers Club and the independent Sunday Speaking group.
- **Nervous about speaking?** Södertörn's *Våga tala: praktiska övningar* is designed for people who find presentations stressful [[4]](#ref-4).

Notes:
- **Stockholm University** describes *Praktisk retorik* as classical rhetoric applied to professional communication, assessed through presentations, peer feedback and written tasks. It has two parts: oral presentation and rhetoric (7.5 credits) and strategic rhetoric (7.5 credits). The page shows daytime, full-time schedules for autumn 2026 and spring 2027 [[1]](#ref-1). It also says the course is given within the Bachelor's Programme in Education with a focus on leadership and communication, where it sits in term 4 [[21]](#ref-21). - **Södertörn** offers both courses each autumn and spring. *Retorik i praktiken* covers speech planning, argumentation, listening, memory technique and body language, in groups of about 20 [[3]](#ref-3). *Våga tala* builds from simple exercises to a final speech, and about half of each group comes from working life [[4]](#ref-4).
- Södertörn also runs other standalone rhetoric courses, such as *Diskussion och debatt* and *Retorik och demokrati*, and a three-year rhetoric programme [[5]](#ref-5).

## How to choose between them

Start with what you need now. If you have a presentation in a month, a short evening course or a few club meetings will help more than a full university term. If you want depth, feedback over time and credits, a university course is the better fit. If cost matters most, visit a club first. Our guide on [how to choose a public speaking course](/en/choose-a-public-speaking-course) goes through the trade-offs step by step.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I take a public speaking course in English in Stockholm?
Stockholm International Toastmasters meets in English on Wednesdays, and Stockholm Speakers Club runs bilingual meetings on Mondays. The independent Sunday Speaking group runs English workshops on Sundays. All three meet at Folkuniversitetet on Kungstensgatan 45 for most of the year.

### How much does a public speaking course cost in Stockholm?
In our check, Medborgarskolan listed single workshops at 299 SEK and four-evening courses at 2,250 SEK. Folkuniversitetet listed *Retorik i arbetslivet* at 5,975 SEK. Stockholm International and Stockholm Speakers Club let guests visit free.

### Can I study rhetoric at university in Stockholm while working?
Yes. Södertörn's *Retorik i praktiken* and *Våga tala* are both half-time, and the university says about half of the *Våga tala* group comes from working life. Check the teaching times on the course page before you apply.

### Is there a course for people who are very nervous about speaking?
Södertörn's *Våga tala: praktiska övningar* is aimed at people who feel stress or anxiety about presentations, and Medborgarskolan runs a *Våga tala* evening course. If your fear is severe or affects daily life, talk to a doctor or psychologist as well.
