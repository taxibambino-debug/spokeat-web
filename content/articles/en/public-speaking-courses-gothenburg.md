# Public speaking courses in Gothenburg

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

Gothenburg is a strong city for learning to speak. The University of Gothenburg runs several rhetoric courses, including one designed for people who already work. The city also has an active Toastmasters scene, with clubs meeting in the evening, at lunchtime and on Saturday mornings, several of them with an online option.

This page lists every option we could verify on 16 November 2026. Each listing links to the provider's own page. We show a price only when the provider publishes it. Listings are grouped by type and ordered alphabetically, not ranked.

For other cities and online options, see the [national directory of public speaking courses](/en/public-speaking-courses-sweden).

## Key takeaways
- **For working professionals:** the University of Gothenburg's *Retorik för yrkesverksamma* is half-time with afternoon sessions, aimed at people who speak as part of their job [[2]](#ref-2).
- **For depth:** *Retorik, grundkurs* is a full 30-credit term that also covers nerves, voice and memory technique [[1]](#ref-1).
- **For a short start:** Folkuniversitetet listed a *Våga tala* course in Gothenburg starting 1 December 2026 at 1,125 SEK [[6]](#ref-6).
- **For regular practice:** five Toastmasters clubs in the Gothenburg area are open to the public. Gothenburg Toastmasters Club meets in English [[9]](#ref-9).
- **For teams:** Göteborgs Tekniska College runs an 8-hour company course, *Speech: Effective communication*, at Torslanda, Lindholmen or your workplace [[17]](#ref-17).

## How to choose between them

If you speak at work and want structured feedback over several weeks, *Retorik för yrkesverksamma* is the most direct fit, provided you have 90 university credits. If you do not, a Toastmasters club gives you regular practice for little money. If you mainly want to get past the fear of standing up, start with a short beginner course or a guest visit to a club. Our guide on [how to choose a public speaking course](/en/choose-a-public-speaking-course) covers this in more detail.

- **Independent.** No provider paid to be listed, and no listing is sponsored.
- **Criteria.** Each listing teaches speaking, presentation or rhetoric, is open to the public (or to anyone meeting standard university eligibility), and has its own page that we opened. We left out events only for enrolled students, company clubs with restricted membership, and providers we could only find on third-party sites or whose page did not load.
- **Facts.** Format, level, length and language come from the provider's page. "Not stated" means the page did not say. Prices appear only where the provider published them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I take a public speaking course in English in Gothenburg?
Yes. Gothenburg Toastmasters Club meets in English on Monday evenings (even weeks) at Örngatan 6, and your first two visits are free. The University of Gothenburg's rhetoric courses we found are taught in Swedish. Göteborgs Tekniska College's company course *Speech: Effective communication* lists basic English as a requirement, and NobleProg's Gothenburg course pages are in English.

### Is there a rhetoric course for working professionals in Gothenburg?
The University of Gothenburg runs *Retorik för yrkesverksamma*, a half-time course with afternoon sessions for people who speak as part of their job. You need at least 90 university credits to be eligible.

### How much does a public speaking course cost in Gothenburg?
Folkuniversitetet listed its *Våga tala* course at 1,125 SEK. The university course pages do not publish fees. Gothenburg Toastmasters Club lets you visit twice free before you decide to join.

### When can I apply for the University of Gothenburg's rhetoric courses?
For spring courses, the main application period is 15 September to 15 October, and for autumn courses 15 March to 15 April. For spring 2027, late application to *Retorik, grundkurs* and *Retorik för yrkesverksamma* opens on 15 December 2026.
