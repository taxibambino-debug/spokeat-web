# Speaking opportunities in Uppsala: universities, student nations and networks

> **Written by** [Ted Scheiman](/authors/ted-scheiman), banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. [How we research and fact-check](/about/editorial-policy)

Uppsala is a university city first. Two universities, six student unions at Uppsala University alone and 13 student nations shape most of the audiences you can reach. Add a chamber of commerce office, business associations and a few active meetups, and you have a compact market that is easy to work from Stockholm too.

The challenge is the same as everywhere: finding the person who decides. At Uppsala University that may be a programme manager, a course coordinator or a department's collaboration lead. At a student organisation it is a committee that changes every year.

This page lists real organisations in Uppsala that host external speakers or describe ways for professionals to contribute. Each entry links to the organisation's own page, and we checked every one on 17 November 2026. We do not promise that any of them has a slot for you. We describe where opportunities typically come from, so you can decide where to pitch.

## Key takeaways

- Uppsala University's Faculty of Science and Technology invites companies to give guest lectures and names programme managers as the contact for planning them [[1]](#ref-1).
- Alumni have a clear route: the university lists "become a guest lecturer" among ways to engage, and publishes a toolkit for alumni lectures of about 35 minutes plus Q&A [[4]](#ref-4) [[5]](#ref-5).
- Student unions such as Uppsalaekonomerna co-host talks with departments, while the 13 nations are mainly social [[6]](#ref-6) [[7]](#ref-7).
- UF Uppsala runs a weekly lecture programme on international affairs [[8]](#ref-8).
- Outside the universities, the Stockholm Chamber of Commerce has an Uppsala office with lunches and roundtables, and Uppsala Tech Meetup lists 55 past evenings of talks [[11]](#ref-11) [[13]](#ref-13).

## How to read this list

- Every organisation below was checked on its own official page on 17 November 2026.
- Entries are grouped by type and ordered alphabetically within each group. This is not a ranking.
- "What the page says" summarises what the organisation publishes. Where a page does not mention guest speakers, we say so.
- Events and calls change. Always check the linked page before you write.

## Universities

For course types, timing and how to reach a course coordinator in general, see our guide on [becoming a guest lecturer at a university](/en/become-a-guest-lecturer-at-a-university). The approach is the same in Uppsala as in [Stockholm](/en/speaking-opportunities-stockholm), [Gothenburg](/en/speaking-opportunities-gothenburg) or [Malmö and Lund](/en/speaking-opportunities-malmo-lund).

### Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU), Ultuna

**What the page says:** SLU's page for organisations that want to work with students covers degree projects, placements, a free Career Connect portal and careers fairs run by the student unions, such as the Ultuna Careers Day. It does not mention guest lectures. SLU Career is the general contact, and heads of subject are listed on department pages [[9]](#ref-9).

**What this means for you:** There is no published guest-lecture route. If your field is agriculture, forestry, food, environment or veterinary medicine, find the relevant course on SLU's site and contact the course leader directly. The student unions' careers days are the other way in.

[SLU: Collaborate with students](https://www.slu.se/en/collaboration/collaborate-with-students/) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Uppsala University: Department of Information Technology

**What the page says:** The department lists guest lectures among its collaboration activities and says many of its courses are run in close cooperation with companies and other actors in society. About 75 per cent of its degree projects are done with companies or organisations. A collaboration head of department (samverkansprefekt) is the named role for industry collaboration [[2]](#ref-2).

[Uppsala University: Collaboration at the Department of Information Technology](https://www.uu.se/institution/informationsteknologi/samverkan) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Uppsala University: Faculty of Science and Technology

**What the page says:** Companies can visit to tell students what it is like to work at their workplace. Suggested topics include working in projects, research, development or production, case studies, entrepreneurship, patents, quality assurance and regulatory requirements. Programme managers (programansvariga) are the contact for planning guest lectures and study visits [[1]](#ref-1).

**What this means for you:** This is the clearest published invitation in Uppsala. Pick the programme closest to your field, then write to its programme manager with a specific topic.

[Uppsala University: Utbildningssamverkan](https://www.uu.se/vetenskapsomrade/teknik-och-naturvetenskap/samverkan/naringsliv-och-samhalle/utbildningssamverkan) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Uppsala University: alumni and central collaboration pages

**What the page says:** The university's central page on working with students covers recruitment, the student-run consultancy Consor, the staffing firm Uppstuk and careers fairs run by student associations. It does not mention guest lectures [[3]](#ref-3). The alumni page is more direct. It lists "become a guest lecturer" and "give a speech at a graduation ceremony" among ways to engage, and says alumni can contribute directly to their own programme or department [[4]](#ref-4).

A staff toolkit for alumni lectures describes the format: a short welcome, a lecture of about 35 minutes, about 20 minutes of moderated questions and a mingle. It recommends that an alumni association, a department and a student association organise the lecture together [[5]](#ref-5).

**What this means for you:** If you studied at Uppsala, start with the alumni team or your old department. The toolkit tells you what a typical slot looks like before you pitch. Our guide on [speaking at your old university](/en/speak-at-your-old-university) covers the approach.

[Uppsala University: Engagera dig som alumn](https://www.uu.se/alumn/engagera-dig) (checked 17 November 2026)

## Student unions, nations and associations

Student organisations plan term by term and change boards often. Our guide on [speaking at student societies](/en/speak-at-student-societies) covers timing and tone.

### Student nations (13 nations)

**What the page says:** Uppsala University lists 13 nations: Gotlands, Gästrike-Hälsinge, Göteborgs, Kalmar, Norrlands, Smålands, Stockholms, Södermanlands-Nerikes, Uplands, Värmlands, Västgöta, Västmanlands-Dala and Östgöta. In the university's words, the nations organise events and social activities, while the unions work to improve the study environment. The page does not mention lectures [[7]](#ref-7).

**What this means for you:** Nations are mainly social. A talk can still fit, especially if you have a link to the nation's home region, but treat them as a secondary target compared with unions and subject associations.

[Uppsala University: Nationer och kårer](https://www.uu.se/utbildning/plugga-i-uppsala/nationer-och-karer) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Student unions at Uppsala University

**What the page says:** The same page lists six unions: Uppsala studentkår, Farmacevtiska Studentkåren, Uppsala teknolog- och naturvetarkår, Uppsalaekonomerna, Juridiska föreningen and Gotlands studentkår Rindi [[7]](#ref-7). As an example of what a union can co-host, the Department of Business Studies held a conversation with a Scania executive vice president on 21 May 2026 "in cooperation with Uppsalaekonomerna", in English, at Ekonomikum [[6]](#ref-6). The university also notes that student associations run targeted careers fairs each year [[3]](#ref-3).

**What this means for you:** Match the union to your field: business and economics to Uppsalaekonomerna, law to Juridiska föreningen, engineering and science to the technology and science union. Ask for the committee that handles corporate relations or events.

[Uppsala University: Nationer och kårer](https://www.uu.se/utbildning/plugga-i-uppsala/nationer-och-karer) (checked 17 November 2026)

### UF Uppsala (Utrikespolitiska föreningen i Uppsala)

**What the page says:** The lecture group organises "thought-provoking talks on a wide range of international topics" and its head oversees "one event each week throughout the year". Past lecturers include "environmental activists, journalists, ambassadors, and politicians". The committee's postal and visiting addresses are on the page [[8]](#ref-8).

**What this means for you:** If your work touches international affairs, trade, security or development, this is one of the most regular lecture programmes in the city.

[UF Uppsala: Our lectures](https://ufuppsala.se/lecture/) (checked 17 November 2026)

## Business networks and innovation

### Företagarna Uppsala län

**What the page says:** The regional branch of Företagarna, the Swedish association for business owners. Its local associations are in Bålsta, Enköping, Heby, Håbo, Tierp, Uppsala and Östhammar. Events listed on the check date included after-work meetings, a business day in Enköping and a seminar on the geopolitical situation [[12]](#ref-12).

**What this means for you:** Local business associations hold breakfast and evening meetings that need content. A practical talk for small business owners fits well. See our guide to [speaking at industry associations and chambers](/en/speak-at-industry-associations-and-chambers).

[Företagarna Uppsala län](https://www.foretagarna.se/regioner/uppsala-lan/) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Stockholm Chamber of Commerce, Uppsala office

**What the page says:** The chamber "maintains its own office and meeting space in central Uppsala". Activities range from Uppsala lunches and roundtable discussions to tailor-made events. Its Property Network meets at least four times a year and has about 80 member companies. Its flagship event, Distinget, is described as a meeting point for politics, academia and business [[11]](#ref-11).

[Stockholm Chamber of Commerce: Uppsala](https://stockholmshandelskammare.se/en/uppsala/) (checked 17 November 2026)

### UU Innovation: Networking Breakfast

**What the page says:** A breakfast with a talk of up to 30 minutes including questions, held in the Segerstedt Building. "Each networking breakfast features a UU Innovation alumnus who shares key insights, lessons learned, and personal highlights." [[10]](#ref-10)

**What this means for you:** The speaker slot is reserved for founders who have been through UU Innovation, so it is not an open call. It is still a good way to meet Uppsala's research and startup community before you pitch elsewhere.

[UU Innovation: Networking Breakfast](https://www.uu.se/en/staff/gateway/research/innovation-support/insight-and-inspiration/networking-breakfast) (checked 17 November 2026)

## Meetups and community events

### Uppsala International Hub and TEAMUP

**What the page says:** Uppsala International Hub is "a community for international talents and their families" that runs more than 150 activities a year. TEAMUP 2026, a recruitment event run by Drivhuset Uppsala, took place on 2 November 2026 at Uppsala Konsert & Kongress. The page does not mention speakers [[15]](#ref-15).

**What this means for you:** The Hub's events calendar is worth watching if your topic helps international professionals build a career in Sweden.

[Uppsala International Hub: TEAMUP 2026](https://internationalhub.uppsala.se/about-uppsala-international-hub/events/teamup2026/) (checked 17 November 2026)

### Uppsala Tech Meetup

**What the page says:** A broad tech group with 1,662 members. "Instead of focusing on a single technology, this is a broader group." It lists 55 past events and no upcoming ones on the check date; the latest was "Agentic Coding - AI workflows #40" in October [[13]](#ref-13). A typical evening, "Real world AI in Uppsala #37" in October 2025, had two talks from local companies after drinks and networking [[14]](#ref-14). The group page has no call for speakers.

**What this means for you:** Talks come from local practitioners and host companies. Attend one evening, then ask the organisers whether they need speakers for the next.

[Uppsala Tech Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/uppsala-tech-meetup/) (checked 17 November 2026)

## How to approach organisers in Uppsala

1. **Pick the audience first.** A science or engineering topic fits a programme at the Faculty of Science and Technology. A foreign-policy topic fits UF Uppsala. A small-business topic fits a Företagarna association.
2. **Find the person who decides.** Programme managers and course coordinators at the university, the events or corporate relations committee at a union.
3. **Use the semester rhythm.** Swedish universities plan teaching well ahead. Our guide to the [academic calendar for guest lectures](/en/academic-calendar-guest-lectures) explains when to write.
4. **Write one short, specific pitch.** Name the course or event, the topic, the length and what the audience will take away.
5. **Follow up once, then stop.** If there is no reply after one polite reminder, move on.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I give a guest lecture at Uppsala University if I am not an alumnus?
Yes. The Faculty of Science and Technology invites companies in general to give guest lectures and names programme managers as the contact [[1]](#ref-1). The Department of Information Technology also lists guest lectures among its collaboration formats [[2]](#ref-2).

### Do Uppsala's student nations book external speakers?
The university describes the nations as organisers of events and social activities and does not mention lectures [[7]](#ref-7). Student unions and associations such as UF Uppsala are more likely hosts for a professional talk [[8]](#ref-8).

### How long is an alumni lecture at Uppsala University?
The university's toolkit suggests a lecture of about 35 minutes, followed by about 20 minutes of moderated questions and a mingle, about 1 hour 40 minutes in total [[5]](#ref-5).

### Are speaking slots in Uppsala paid?
None of the pages we checked mention fees. Assume unpaid unless the organiser says otherwise, and see our guide on [finding speaking engagements without an agency](/en/find-speaking-engagements-without-an-agency).
