Why speaking is worth your time

Every talk compounds. It shapes how your field sees you, gives you material to share and makes you sharper at what you do.

01

Build industry authority

Being invited to teach or speak signals that you are a reference in your field. People remember who explained it to them.

02

Earn prestigious, real-world credentials

"Guest lecturer at a business school" or "speaker at an industry association" carries weight on your profile, CV and bio.

03

Stand out with a distinctive profile

Most professionals only post. Speaking gives you a point of view in front of a live audience that few of your peers have.

04

Create content from every talk

Recordings, photos, slides and the questions you get become posts, articles and material for your own channels.

05

Sharpen your thinking

Explaining your work to a room forces clarity. Questions from students and peers show you what really matters.

06

Grow your presentation skills

Low-stakes settings like meetups and societies are the best training ground before bigger stages.

07

Widen your network

Meet organisers, faculty, peers and decision makers who would never have found you otherwise.

08

Attract talent to your team

Students and young professionals get to know you and your company. That is employer branding money cannot buy.

09

Open the next doors

Talks lead to more talks, and to podcast invitations, media requests, advisory roles and board seats.

Show it

Every talk becomes proof you can share

When a talk is agreed, you get a share page and a ready-made post image for LinkedIn. Announce it before, share it after, and let the people who follow you see your expertise in action.

Questions

Is this for paid speaking gigs?

No. This is for professionals who want visibility, experience and a stronger profile. We do not search for or promote paid speaking work.

Who sends the email?

You choose. We can send it for you with you in copy, and replies come straight to you. Or you copy the draft and send it yourself.

What happens if an organiser does not reply?

We send one polite follow-up after a week. If there is still no answer, we pause that contact until next term and never go around them to a colleague. Instead we suggest other courses, student societies at the same university and similar opportunities elsewhere, ready for you to approve.

Will organisers be flooded with requests?

No. Each organiser receives at most two pitches through us per month, and anyone can opt out at any time.

What does it cost?

Searching and seeing your matches is free. You buy an outreach pack when you are ready to reach out, with no subscription. If no organiser replies within 30 days of your last email sent through us, the pack is refunded. Public calls for speakers are always free to apply to, and there is never a fee when an organiser says yes. Prices are published before launch.

Do you scrape my LinkedIn?

No. You upload LinkedIn’s own "Save to PDF" export or your CV, and you confirm everything we use.
