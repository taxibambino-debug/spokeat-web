Why choose us

Finding the right stage takes research, timing and the right introduction. We do that work. Searching and public calls for speakers are free, and you only pay when you want us to reach an organiser for you.

Strong local network

We build relationships with universities, business schools, student societies, chambers and communities in every market we serve. We know their course calendars and approach them in their own language.

No replies, no charge

If none of the organisers in your outreach pack replies within 30 days, you get your money back. Applies when we send the emails for you.

Self-driving, with you in control

Matching and outreach run automatically: we find the opportunities, identify the right person and write the email. Nothing is sent until you approve it.
