Guest lecturing in Stockholm: universities and business schools that invite practitioners

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

You have ten or fifteen years of practice behind you and want to bring it into a Stockholm lecture hall. The question is where to start. Some institutions publish a clear route for guest lecturers. Others leave it to individual teachers. Most do not say when to get in touch.

This guide covers four Stockholm institutions whose own pages invite practitioners in: Stockholm University, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, the Stockholm School of Economics (SSE) and Berghs School of Communication. For each, we explain the route it publishes, who the route is for and what kind of teaching or event you would join. We also show how Stockholm University's term is structured, because timing decides whether your email lands.

We checked every page on 16 November 2026. For the full list of Stockholm speaking venues, including student societies, chambers and meetups, see speaking opportunities in Stockholm.

Key takeaways

  • Stockholm University's alumni office matches alumni guest lecturers with a student audience. Course leaders at many departments want practitioners.
  • KTH runs an alumni guest lecture register with KTH Career Service. Non-alumni can contact the KTH Student Union or the teacher of a specific programme.
  • SSE's Tech Initiative runs tech talks in several formats and openly invites future contributors.
  • Berghs invites international practitioners passing through Sweden to guest lecture.
  • Stockholm University splits each term into four parts. Pitch before the part your target course runs in.

Who invites practitioners, and how

Stockholm University: alumni matched to courses

Stockholm University's alumni page is direct. It says guest lecturers who can share real-world experience are "in high demand", both by students and by "academic advisors and course leaders at many departments" [1].

The route is simple. You email alumni@su.se with an idea of what you want to present. The alumni office says it will "do our best to match you with the right student audience" [1]. This means you do not have to find the right course yourself. It also means you depend on the office to find a fit.

The route is written for alumni. If you did not study there, approach a course directly. Our guide on finding the right course for a guest lecture shows how.

Stockholm University offers more than 100 master's programmes taught in English, across science, the humanities, social sciences and law [2]. That gives English-speaking practitioners a wide range of possible courses.

KTH: alumni register, student union or programme teacher

KTH publishes two routes.

For alumni. KTH Alumni runs a register where you record your interest in guest lecturing. The page says KTH organises guest lectures together with KTH Career Service, in which alumni share their professional experience and career journeys. Questions go to alumni@kth.se [3].

For everyone else. KTH's page on collaborating with students says a guest speaker helps "strengthen the link between our degree programmes and industry". You lecture on a specific subject while representing your industry. To do this, you can contact the Student Union at naringsliv@ths.kth.se, or contact the teacher on a specific programme [4].

The same page lists study visits as another option: you host students at your workplace instead of going to campus [4]. If your work is easier to show than to describe, that may suit you better.

KTH offers 60 master's degree programmes in English [5].

Stockholm School of Economics: SSE Tech Initiative talks

SSE's route is not a general guest lecture scheme. It is a talk series. The SSE Tech Initiative helps students "become technology-enabled leaders" and "organizes talks with entrepreneurs, executives, and thought leaders", plus seminars, workshops and bootcamps [6].

Its list of past tech talks shows the range of formats: lunch lectures, panel discussions, workshops, keynotes and Q&A conversations. Past speakers came from companies including Spotify, Google, Mastercard, Tink, Scania, Microsoft Sweden and IBM Sweden [7].

The current series, "Technology in an uncertain world", is run with SSE's Center for Security and Resilience. It covers AI, autonomous systems, startups, cybersecurity and the ethics of security technology. The page invites people working on technological innovation, security or resilience to contribute to future editions, at techinitiative@hhs.se [8].

This suits you if your topic is technology and business, and you can frame it as a talk rather than a slot in a course.

Berghs School of Communication: international practitioners

Berghs teaches communication, marketing and design. In an article from March 2025, it says it aims to bring in the best guest speakers in the industry, from Sweden and abroad. The article ends: "Passing through Sweden and interested in delivering a guest lecture to Berghs students? Don't hesitate to reach out!" [9]

Berghs does not publish a guest lecture inbox. The article links to a "Contact us" option, and lists the switchboard, +46 8-587 550 00 [9].

How Stockholm University's terms run

A course coordinator plans the term before it starts. If you write when the course is already running, the schedule is usually full. So it helps to know the calendar.

Stockholm University publishes these dates [10]:

Term Dates Parts
Autumn 2026 31 August 2026 to 17 January 2027 A from 31 Aug, B from 1 Oct, C from 3 Nov, D from 3 Dec
Spring 2027 18 January 2027 to 6 June 2027 A from 18 Jan, B from 22 Feb, C from 29 Mar, D from 3 May

Each term has two periods, each split into two parts (A and B, then C and D) [10]. Check the course page to see which part your target course runs in. For example, if it runs in part C of spring 2027, starting 29 March, aim to reach the coordinator well before that, ideally during the previous term.

These are Stockholm University's dates. KTH, SSE and Berghs set their own schedules, so check each institution. Our guide to the academic calendar for guest lectures explains how to time a pitch across Swedish institutions.

Which courses and formats use practitioners

The pages we checked point to four kinds of teaching where practitioners fit:

  1. Programme courses with an industry link. KTH describes guest lecturing as teaching a specific subject while representing your industry [4]. Approach the programme teacher.
  2. Courses across many departments. Stockholm University says course leaders at many departments want guest lecturers [1]. The alumni office does the matching.
  3. Career-focused sessions. KTH's alumni lectures are about professional experience and career journeys, organised with Career Service [3].
  4. Open talk series. SSE's Tech Initiative runs lunch lectures, panels, workshops and keynotes outside the course timetable [7].

To decide which fits you, ask: do I have a teachable subject, a career story or a current debate? A subject fits a course. A career story fits a career session. A debate fits a talk series.

How to approach a Stockholm course

  1. Pick the route. Use the alumni route if you are an alumnus. If not, use the published non-alumni route or find the course coordinator.
  2. Name the course. A pitch that names a course and a learning goal is easier to say yes to. See how to become a guest lecturer at a university.
  3. Offer one clear session. Give a title, three points and a length. Offer to adapt to the course.
  4. Send early. Aim for the term before the course runs.
  5. Follow up once. If there is no reply, send one polite follow-up. Our email templates for guest lectures include one.

What we could not confirm

  • Payment. None of the four institutions' pages state whether guest lecturers are paid or receive expenses. Ask before you agree.
  • Selection criteria and response times. None of the pages describe them.
  • Term dates for KTH, SSE and Berghs. We only checked Stockholm University's calendar for this guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I guest lecture at Stockholm University if I did not study there?

The published route is for alumni. If you are not an alumnus, contact the course coordinator of a relevant course directly, with a specific session idea.

How do I guest lecture at KTH without being an alumnus?

KTH's own page says you can contact the KTH Student Union at naringsliv@ths.kth.se, or the teacher on a specific programme.

When should I contact a Stockholm course coordinator?

Before the term or part in which the course runs. At Stockholm University, spring 2027 starts on 18 January 2027 and is split into parts starting 18 January, 22 February, 29 March and 3 May.

Do Stockholm universities pay guest lecturers?

The pages we checked do not say. Ask the coordinator before you agree, and do not assume either way.

Can I lecture in English?

Stockholm University offers more than 100 master's programmes taught in English, and KTH offers 60. Berghs and the SSE Tech Initiative also publish their guest speaker pages in English.
