Public speaking courses in Malmö and Lund

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

You want to speak better in front of a group, and you live or work in Malmö or Lund. The options look scattered: university courses with credits, evening courses at study associations, speaking clubs and private trainers. They differ a lot in cost, time and what you get out of them.

This directory lists the options we could verify on each provider's own page. Each listing shows the type, format, level, length, language and, only where the provider publishes it, the price. We checked every listing on 16 November 2026.

If you have already done a course and want a stage to practise on, see our guide to speaking opportunities in Malmö and Lund.

Key takeaways

  • Lund University runs the widest range of credit-bearing rhetoric courses in the region, from a 3-credit course in English to full-time 30-credit semesters in Swedish.
  • Malmö University offers a short summer course in presentation technique through drama, taught in English, plus a distance course in online presentation skills.
  • Malmö and Lund each have a Toastmasters club that meets every other week. Malmö Toastmasters says guests can watch without being asked to speak.
  • Short evening courses run through Folkuniversitetet in Malmö and Medborgarskolan in Ystad and Kristianstad, with published prices. NobleProg runs private presentation courses from Malmö.
  • University courses are free for citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland. Others pay tuition fees set per course.

University courses (credit-bearing)

University courses give higher education credits (hp). You apply through the national application service. Lund University's pages refer applicants to antagning.se, and fee-paying applicants to UniversityAdmissions.se [5]. Citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland pay no application or tuition fees. Other applicants pay a fee per course, shown below where published [3].

Lund University describes its rhetoric courses as training in "the art of persuasion in speech and writing": you learn to open a speech, give celebration and defence speeches, and argue for and against a question [8].

Contract education through your employer

Lund University sells further education to employers and public authorities (uppdragsutbildning). The employer pays and registers you. Private individuals cannot buy it on their own [21]. If your organisation wants a tailored rhetoric course, this is the route to ask about.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free public speaking course in Malmö or Lund?

University courses at Lund University and Malmö University are free for citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland. You need to meet the entry requirements and apply by the deadline. Toastmasters clubs let you visit as a guest; ask the club about membership costs.

Can I take a rhetoric course in English in Lund or Malmö?

Yes. At Lund University, RETB03 and RETN10 are taught in English. At Malmö University, AK102E and AK107E list English as the language of instruction. Folkuniversitetet in Malmö runs Public Speaking in English.

When do I apply for a spring course at Lund University?

Lund University says applications for its spring rhetoric course RETA32 are normally open from mid-September to mid-October. Some courses also open for late application. Check the course page for the exact round.

Which option suits a busy professional?

Practical Rhetoric (RETA14) at Lund runs at half pace in the afternoons. Short courses at Folkuniversitetet and Medborgarskolan take one evening a week for four to six weeks. Toastmasters meets every other week in the early evening.
