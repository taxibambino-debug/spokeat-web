University courses in rhetoric and public speaking

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

A university course in rhetoric gives you more than tips. You get theory, structured practice, assessed speeches and higher education credits on your record. For citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland, Swedish universities charge no tuition.

The catch is admin. Each course has its own level, pace, entry requirements and application round. This page collects that information for credit-bearing rhetoric and public speaking courses at Swedish universities, as each university publishes it. We checked every listing on 16 November 2026.

Many of these courses are taught in Swedish. Courses in English are marked.

Key takeaways

  • Several universities run short, practice-focused courses of 3 to 15 credits that fit around a job, often at half or quarter pace.
  • Linköping University runs its introductory speaking course both on campus and as a distance course, and runs a public speaking course in English.
  • Full-semester introductory courses of 30 credits exist at Lund University, Södertörn University, the University of Gothenburg and Uppsala University.
  • Application rounds usually close months before the course starts. At the University of Gothenburg, autumn courses list 16 March to 15 April, and spring courses list 15 September to 15 October.
  • Some listed courses have been cancelled or are being phased out. Always check the current page.

How to read the listings

  • Credits (hp): Swedish higher education credits. Full-time study for one term is 30 credits.
  • Level: First cycle (grundnivå) needs general entry requirements, sometimes more. Second cycle (avancerad nivå) usually needs a bachelor's degree or a set number of credits.
  • Pace: 100% is full-time. 50% is half pace, 25% is quarter pace.
  • Application: Courses list an application period or a late application date. "Late application" means you can still apply after the main deadline, if places remain.
  • Fees: Citizens of EU/EEA countries and Switzerland pay no application or tuition fees, as stated on, for example, Lund University's course pages [20]. Fees for others vary by course.

Within each type below, universities and courses are in alphabetical order. We do not rank them.

Våga tala is for anyone who wants to become more comfortable speaking, with groups of about 20, of whom about half come from working life [34]. Retorik i praktiken covers argumentation, speech planning and different speaking situations [36]. - RE010G at Örebro applies classical and modern rhetoric to public speaking, debating and media. It requires general entry requirements and is taught in English [35]. We did not find a Swedish-language freestanding rhetoric course at Örebro on the pages we could open.

Stockholm-area note

Two Stockholm-area universities feature on this page. Södertörn University's courses above are open freestanding courses. Stockholm University's Praktisk retorik is listed as part of a programme, so check whether it is open to you before you plan around it [40].

Contract education for employers

Universities also sell rhetoric training to employers. Uppsala University's Department of Literature and Rhetoric offers courses such as "Retorik och presentationsteknik", adapted to the group, in Uppsala or at the client's site. Prices are given on request [32]. This route only works if your employer pays.

Frequently asked questions

Can I study rhetoric at university while working full-time?

Yes, if you choose a part-time course. Several courses run at quarter or half pace, some in the afternoon or evening, and Linköping University runs some as distance courses. Check the teaching times on the course page before you apply.

Do I need a degree to take a rhetoric course at university?

Not for most first-cycle courses, which need general entry requirements. Some courses need more: the University of Gothenburg's Retorik för yrkesverksamma requires 90 credits, and second-cycle courses usually require a bachelor's degree.

When is the application deadline for rhetoric courses?

It depends on the university and term. The University of Gothenburg lists 15 April for autumn and 15 October for spring. Lund University says applications for its spring course RETA32 normally run from mid-September to mid-October. Some courses open for late application if places remain.

Are there university rhetoric courses in English in Sweden?

Yes. Examples include Linköping University's Tala effektivt inför andra i internationella sammanhang (738G40), Lund University's RETB03 and RETN10, Malmö University's AK102E and AK107E, and Örebro University's RE010G.
