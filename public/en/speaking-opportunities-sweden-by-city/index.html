Speaking opportunities in Sweden, city by city

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

Most advice about speaking opportunities assumes you live in Stockholm. But Sweden has universities, student unions, incubators and business networks in every region, and smaller cities often have fewer people competing for the same slots.

This hub does two things. First, it points you to our detailed guides for Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö and Lund, and Uppsala. Second, it gives short, verified starting points for seven more university cities: Jönköping, Karlstad, Linköping, Luleå, Umeå, Växjö and Kalmar, and Örebro.

Every organisation below links to its own page, and we checked each one on 17 November 2026. The sections for smaller cities are deliberately short. Where we could not find a published route for guest speakers, we say so instead of guessing.

Key takeaways

  • Several universities publish an explicit invitation. Jönköping International Business School (JIBS) has a "Study visit or guest speaker" page, and Umeå's business school asks alumni to give talks about their career path [13] [9].
  • Linköping University lists guest lecturing among the ways alumni can engage, and runs a monthly lunch forum where university and business meet [2] [3].
  • Luleå University of Technology offers a campus meeting place where organisations can host inspiration events for students [7].
  • Incubators are a nationwide network: SISP lists 29 incubators and 31 science parks among its members [21].
  • In every city, student unions and their sections run career days and are often the fastest way to an audience [1] [7].

How to read the city sections

  • Cities are in alphabetical order. Within each city, entries are grouped by type. This is not a ranking.
  • Each entry summarises what the organisation's own page says on 17 November 2026.
  • Events and calls change. Always check the linked page before you write.

Jönköping

Jönköping International Business School (JIBS)

What the page says: JIBS invites organisations to host a study visit and offers an alternative: "someone from your company/organisation can come and hold a guest lecture/speech at JIBS." Listed benefits include "Strengthen your brand" and "Exchange knowledge and expertise". Two coordinators are named as contacts [13]. A separate page lists courses that take company challenges, with registration windows such as October to December for courses that run January to March [14].

JIBS: Study visit or guest speaker (checked 17 November 2026)

Science Park Jönköping

What the page says: A meeting place for entrepreneurship, innovation and creativity "in 13 different locations" across the county. The events page showed no events on the check date. It offers free sessions with a business developer [15].

Science Park Jönköping: Events (checked 17 November 2026)

Karlstad

Karlstad University

What the page says: The page on working with students lists four routes: a career portal, guidance for companies and organisations, mentoring, and "Gästföreläsningar och studiebesök" (guest lectures and study visits). The general contact is the university's main email and phone [16].

What this means for you: Guest lectures are a recognised format. Start from that page, then find the course closest to your field and contact the course coordinator.

Karlstad University: Samarbeta med studenter (checked 17 November 2026)

Linköping (and Norrköping)

Linköping University (LiU)

What the page says: LiU's alumni page has a section headed "Bli mentor eller gästföreläsare" (become a mentor or guest lecturer). It suggests contacting LiU Alumni or the programme, section or association you are interested in directly [2]. For non-alumni, LiU's page on meeting students points to career days run by student unions and sections, usually via each section's business liaison [1].

LiU Lunchklubben: A lunchtime forum where "universitet, näringsliv och samhälle" (university, business and society) meet, run with Linköping Science Park, Norrköping Science Park and Region Östergötland. The autumn 2026 theme was AI, with sessions in Linköping and Norrköping [3].

LiU: Engagera dig som alumn (checked 17 November 2026)

LEAD

What the page says: A not-for-profit business incubator in Linköping and Norrköping, wholly owned by LiU Holding [5]. Its past events include LEAD Talks, lunch lectures (lunchföreläsning), seminars and Venture Arena [4].

LEAD: Previous events (checked 17 November 2026)

Luleå

Luleå University of Technology (LTU)

What the page says: LTU's "Meeting Point" in the B building is an open meeting place where organisations can host inspiration events and present themselves to students; booking is via a form. Career fairs are run by the student unions, including LARV through Teknologkåren [7]. LTU's mentoring programme includes joint sessions "med föreläsningar, nätverkande och erfarenhetsutbyte" (with lectures, networking and exchange of experience), and the collaboration office at samverkan@ltu.se helps organisations find the right format [6]. The collaboration page also listed open seminars and a digital morning meeting on sustainable business in November 2026 [8].

LTU: Träffa studenterna (checked 17 November 2026)

Umeå

Umeå School of Business, Economics and Statistics (USBE)

What the page says: USBE asks alumni to "Give a talk about your professional journey from studies to today with a focus on your current professional role." Sessions can be online, and "Guest lectures within courses can of course also be more detailed and technical." [9]

USBE: Alumni at USBE (checked 17 November 2026)

Umeå University: collaboration and Uminova

What the page says: The Research Support and Collaboration Office helps partners "explore ways to engage with our students and researchers". Uminova Innovation aims to "stimulate an exchange of knowledge, ideas and experience between the University and the industry". Guest lectures are not mentioned on this page [10].

Umeå University: Collaboration and innovation (checked 17 November 2026)

Tech meetups and conferences in Umeå

What the pages say: Umedev is a free one-day conference for the IT industry in northern Sweden. Its 2026 call for speakers ran from 24 November 2025 to 15 February 2026, with 40-minute presentations and 20-minute lightning talks, and sessions may not be used to sell [11]. Cloud Native Umeå held an autumn meetup on 23 September 2026 at IceLab, Umeå University, and tried a call for speakers for it [12].

Umedev 2026: Call for Speakers (checked 17 November 2026)

Växjö and Kalmar

Linnaeus University

What the page says: The page we could open describes Global Entrepreneurship Week 2022 in Kalmar and Växjö, a collaboration between Linnaeus University and Drivhuset with lectures, workshops and a Social Innovation Camp, open to students, staff and the public [17].

What this means for you: This shows the kind of events the university runs with outside speakers, but it is from 2022. We could not open a current guest-lecture or collaboration page for Linnaeus University on the check date. Start from the course catalogue and contact course coordinators directly.

Linnaeus University: Global Entrepreneurship Week (checked 17 November 2026)

Örebro

Örebro University

What the page says: Örebro's pages for employers list several ways to meet students, including Campusmässan (a career fair), "Meet a professional", developing ideas with students and Studentuppdrag (paid student assignments) [18]. Professionsprofilen is a format where students meet invited professionals in small round-table conversations rather than lectures [19]. Campusmässan 2025 brought roughly 100 employers together with students from 82 programmes [20].

What this means for you: The published routes favour dialogue and career events over lectures. A round-table at Professionsprofilen is a realistic first step; for a lecture, contact a course coordinator directly.

Örebro University: Studentuppdrag (checked 17 November 2026)

How to search locally in any Swedish city

The same five places exist almost everywhere. Work through them in this order:

  1. The university's collaboration and alumni pages. Search the university's site for "gästföreläsning", "samverka med studenter" and "alumn". If you studied there, the alumni route is usually the easiest. Our guide on becoming a guest lecturer at a university explains how to find the right course.
  2. Student unions and their sections. They run career days and lecture evenings. See our guide on speaking at student societies.
  3. Incubators and science parks. SISP's member list covers incubators and science parks "throughout Sweden – from north to south" [21]. Many run breakfast or lunch talks.
  4. Business networks. Local chambers of commerce, Företagarna associations and service clubs. Rotary District 2365, for example, has about 80 clubs in Västra Götaland, Jönköping County, Kungsbacka and Varberg, and says talks at club meetings are an important part of what clubs do [22]. Our article on overlooked places to speak covers these venues.
  5. Meetups and conference calls. Search Meetup and Sessionize for the city name. Our guide to meetup talks explains how to get a first slot.

For the general method behind all of this, read how to get speaking opportunities.

Frequently asked questions

Which Swedish universities openly invite guest speakers?

Among the pages we checked, JIBS in Jönköping has a dedicated guest speaker page [13], and Karlstad University lists guest lectures and study visits as a collaboration format [16]. Linköping and Umeå's business school invite alumni to give talks [2] [9], and so does Uppsala University (see our Uppsala guide).

Is it worth pitching outside Stockholm?

Often, yes. Smaller cities have the same mix of courses, unions, incubators and networks, and the published routes we found, such as JIBS's guest speaker page and LTU's Meeting Point, are open to organisations in general [13] [7]. Many organisers also accept online sessions; USBE says alumni talks can be online [9].

Do I need to live in the city to speak there?

No. A clear topic and a fit with the audience matter more. Mention in your pitch whether you can come in person or prefer to speak online.

Are these slots paid?

None of the university and network pages we checked mention speaker fees. Assume unpaid unless the organiser says otherwise.
