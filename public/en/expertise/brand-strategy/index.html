Speaking opportunities in brand strategy

Standing out and differentiating in a saturated market.

In markets where more and more offers look alike, the question of how to stand apart matters increasingly to management teams and marketing departments. Audiences have heard positioning theory many times and now want to hear how it works when real decisions are made. Organisers book speakers who have led or closely followed brand work and can talk about the hard choices: what you gave up, how you brought the organisation along and what happened when customers reacted.

Business schools and marketing courses welcome practitioners who can add to the textbook, and advertising and communications networks run seminars and panels. Sector-specific industry days often want someone who can discuss branding in the audience's own terms. Organisers expect clear examples and an explanation of why the strategy worked, not just a reveal of the new logo.

Questions

Can I talk about brand work I did as a consultant?

Yes, if the client agrees or you anonymise the details. Focus on the process and the decisions.

How do I stand out from all the agencies talking about branding?

Be specific about sector and situation. A talk on branding in a particular industry or growth stage draws more interest than general principles.

Can I show client work in a talk?

Only what you are allowed to share. Ask the client or anonymise the case; organisers care more about the lesson than the logo.
