Speaking opportunities for sustainability and ESG experts

Sustainability and ESG have moved from side topic to core business: reporting rules, climate targets, supply chains and circular models now shape strategy and investment. Audiences want practitioners who can say what it takes to deliver, not only why it matters.

Universities run sustainability courses and programmes that welcome practitioners. Student sustainability societies are active at most large universities. Chambers of commerce, sustainability networks and dedicated forums run seminars and annual events, and several international weeks open calls for sessions each year.

Questions

What do sustainability organisers want from practitioners?

Concrete experience of delivery: data, trade-offs, costs and results, including what did not work.

Is ESG reporting too technical for a talk?

Not if you explain what changed in practice. Courses in particular value step-by-step accounts.

How do I avoid greenwashing on stage?

Use verifiable facts, say what is still unsolved and avoid claims you cannot back up.
