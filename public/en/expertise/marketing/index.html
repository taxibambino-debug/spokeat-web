Speaking opportunities for marketing and communication experts

Marketing and communication professionals are in demand on stage because the field changes fast and everyone has an opinion about it. Search, social platforms, AI-generated content, brand and measurement all shift from year to year, and audiences want to hear from people doing the work now, not five years ago.

Marketing courses at universities and business schools use practitioners to show campaigns and results from the inside. Marketing associations, agencies' networks and meetups run regular talks and breakfast seminars. Student marketing societies invite alumni and practitioners. Podcasts and webinars are natural formats, and a good case study travels well between them.

Questions

Can I show client work in a talk?

Only what you are allowed to share. Ask the client or anonymise the case; organisers care more about the lesson than the logo.

Which formats suit marketing talks?

Case-based guest lectures, short talks at breakfast seminars, panels and podcast interviews.

Do I need my own audience to get booked?

No. A clear topic and a relevant case matter more than followers, especially for courses and associations.
