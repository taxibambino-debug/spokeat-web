Speaking opportunities for HR and future-of-work experts

HR and the future of work cover recruitment, employer branding, competence, hybrid work, workplace culture and the rules that frame them. These are everyday questions for every organisation, so the audiences are wide: HR programmes at universities, managers' networks, HR associations and company events.

Universities and university colleges with HR and organisation programmes often build guest lectures into their courses. HR associations and tech-industry networks run seminars on themes such as skills shortages and AI at work. Employer-branding guest lectures double as recruitment visibility, which makes them attractive to employers too.

Questions

Can I speak about HR if I am a manager, not an HR professional?

Yes. Many organisers want the manager's view of hiring, onboarding or hybrid work alongside HR's.

Are employer-branding talks a sales pitch?

They should not be. Students and audiences respond to honest stories about work, not recruitment ads.

Where do HR events publish calls for speakers?

Mostly on association and conference sites; many are invitation-based, which is why direct, specific outreach works.
