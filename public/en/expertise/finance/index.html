Speaking opportunities for finance, fintech and payments experts

Finance, fintech and payments have an unusually rich speaking scene: large international conferences, national industry days, university finance programmes and a steady stream of regulation that people need explained. New rules, new payment rails and digital assets keep creating questions that practitioners can answer better than anyone.

Universities and business schools invite people who work with payments, risk, compliance or banking to show how the textbook meets real operations. Industry associations and fintech networks run seminars and panels. The big conferences open calls for content well ahead, and some charge speakers for a pass, which is worth checking before you apply.

Questions

Do big fintech conferences charge speakers?

Some do, for example through a discounted speaker pass. Our posts show what the speaker pays or gets, so you can decide before you apply.

Can I speak about regulation if I am not a lawyer?

Yes, if you describe how a rule affected your work. Avoid giving legal advice and say where your experience ends.

Which audiences are easiest to start with?

University courses in finance and student finance societies are often open to practitioners and are a good first slot.
