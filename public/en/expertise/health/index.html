Speaking opportunities for health, wellbeing and life-science experts

Health covers a wide range of expertise: life science and medtech, healthcare organisation and digital health, and wellbeing and stress at work. Audiences range from medical and engineering students to industry associations, healthcare conferences and company events.

This is also an area where trust matters most. Talks about health must stay within the speaker's real expertise and rest on evidence. Practitioners from life science, medtech and healthcare management are welcome guests in university courses and industry events. Talks on workplace wellbeing and stress should come from people with relevant professional background, and never replace medical advice.

Questions

Can I speak about stress or wellbeing without being a clinician?

Only within your own role and experience, for example as a manager describing measures you introduced. Clinical advice belongs to licensed professionals.

What do healthcare organisers check?

Background, evidence and conflicts of interest. Be open about your employer and any commercial links.

Are patient cases allowed in talks?

Never share identifiable patient data. Use published or fully anonymised material only.
