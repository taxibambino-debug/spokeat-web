Speaking opportunities for security and risk experts

Security and risk now reach far beyond IT departments. Cyber attacks, fraud, privacy rules and new security requirements affect boards, finance teams and public organisations, and audiences want practitioners who can explain what to do without jargon.

Security conferences and community events such as BSides run open calls. Universities with computer science, law and risk programmes invite practitioners to show real incidents and controls. Industry associations, chambers and breakfast seminars want plain-language talks for leaders who must decide and budget. Panels on resilience and fraud are common at finance and public-sector events.

Questions

Can I talk about real incidents?

Only with permission and without details that create new risk. Many speakers describe a composite or a published case.

Do security conferences want non-technical talks?

Many do, especially on governance, people and risk. Community events are a good first slot.

How do I make security understandable for leaders?

Start with the business impact and one decision they must make, then show what good looks like.
