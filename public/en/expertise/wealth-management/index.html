Speaking opportunities in succession and wealth management

Managing family wealth and passing it on.

Over the coming years many family businesses and family fortunes are due to pass to the next generation. That raises questions about ownership governance, fairness between siblings, tax and how to prepare heirs who have not been part of the business. Organisers look for speakers with first-hand experience of a succession or long practice as advisers, and a talk gets booked when it treats the human conflicts as seriously as the financial ones.

The subject suits family business courses at business schools, seminars run by associations for owner-managed companies and closed-door sessions for family owners. Podcasts on ownership and enterprise cover it too. The talk should be education, not individual advice. Organisers expect you not to market specific products to the audience, since such advice can be regulated, and to disclose whether you work for a bank, an asset manager or an advisory firm.

Questions

Can I talk about my own family's succession?

Yes, if your family agrees. Organisers value personal stories, but leave out details of the wealth itself and of anyone who has not consented.

Can I speak on this as an adviser?

Yes, but state your role clearly and keep the content general. The audience should not take the talk as an offer of your services.

Do big fintech conferences charge speakers?

Some do, for example through a discounted speaker pass. Our posts show what the speaker pays or gets, so you can decide before you apply.
