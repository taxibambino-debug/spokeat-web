Guest lecturing in Malmö and Lund: universities that invite practitioners

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 16 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

A guest lecture is one of the most credible ways to share what you know. Students get a view of real work. You get practice, visibility and a reason to sharpen your thinking.

In Malmö and Lund, the two main universities handle guest lectures in different ways. Lund University's engineering faculty says plainly that its courses welcome guest lecturers. Its business school frames them around partner companies. Malmö University does not describe a guest-lecture route at all on its collaboration page, but some of its courses invite outside practitioners.

This guide explains who decides, which kinds of courses invite practitioners, and how to approach them. Every institution named here was checked on its own page on 16 November 2026. None of this guarantees a slot. It shows where guest lectures typically come from.

Key takeaways

  • The course teacher decides. LTH states that a company "first needs to establish a relationship with the teacher responsible for the course" [1].
  • Central contacts help you find that teacher: LTH has an External Engagement Coordinator, and LUSEM points to Career Services [1] [2].
  • Malmö University's collaboration page lists degree projects, internships and Stormathon, not guest lectures, but MaU Innovation suggests inspirational speakers to lecturers [4] [5].
  • Course pages are your best map. They often name the course coordinator and describe whether students meet outside practitioners [6] [7].

Who decides on a guest lecture

At Swedish universities, each course has a teacher responsible for it, often called the course coordinator (kursansvarig in Swedish). This person plans the sessions and decides whether an outside speaker fits.

LTH, Lund University's Faculty of Engineering, describes the process clearly. Its courses "welcome guest lecturers and/or arrange study visits to companies and organisations", but the company "first needs to establish a relationship with the teacher responsible for the course". An External Engagement Coordinator named on the page can help you find the right programme, course and course coordinator [1].

That pattern is common: a central office points you in the right direction, and the teacher makes the call. Our guide on how to become a guest lecturer at a university explains the general approach.

Institutions in Lund that invite practitioners

Listed alphabetically by unit. Each entry was checked on 16 November 2026.

AI Lund (Lund University)

AI Lund is an open network for AI research, education and innovation at Lund University. Its collaboration page lists, as a way for industry to take part: "Share your real world experience as a guest speaker." [8]

Fits: practitioners who apply AI or machine learning in a company or public body.

AI Lund: Collaborate (checked 16 November 2026)

LTH: Faculty of Engineering (Lund University)

LTH has a dedicated page on guest lectures and study visits, quoted above [1].

Fits: engineers, product and technology leaders, and anyone whose work connects to an engineering course.

LTH: Guest lecture and study visit (checked 16 November 2026)

LUSEM: School of Economics and Management (Lund University)

LUSEM's "Business meets students" page says: "Partner companies can make a meaningful impact by sharing their expertise through guest lectures at LUSEM." It also lists company presentations and roundtables, and refers enquiries to Career Services [2]. Its wider collaboration page adds case work, thesis collaboration and hubs for start-ups and sustainability [3].

Teachers also invite individuals directly. In January 2025, a course director in the Master's Programme in Information Systems invited two London-based designers to give a guest lecture in a course on Human-Computer Interaction design. Their book was the course's main literature [9].

Fits: finance, management, marketing, information systems and sustainability practitioners.

LUSEM: Business meets students (checked 16 November 2026)

VentureLab (Lund University)

VentureLab supports student entrepreneurship at Lund University. Its events page lists past talks and workshops that included a guest talk by a startup co-founder and a lunch talk by a founder and author. Several listings date from 2023 and 2024, so check for current activity [10].

Fits: founders and early-stage operators.

VentureLab: Events (checked 16 November 2026)

Institutions in Malmö that invite practitioners

Malmö University: courses in the School of Arts and Communication (K3)

Some Malmö University course pages describe outside contributors. The master's course "Media and Communication Studies: Understanding the Creative Industries" says: "Students will meet local and/or international creative and cultural actors" who visit to discuss their work [6].

Fits: people working in media, design, culture and the creative industries.

Malmö University: KK641C course page (checked 16 November 2026)

Malmö University: MaU Innovation

MaU Innovation supports teachers. Under external relations, it offers lecturers "suggestions for inspirational speakers to enrich various teaching modules" [5].

Fits: entrepreneurs and innovation practitioners. Making yourself known here may put you on the list teachers draw from.

MaU Innovation: Teaching support (checked 16 November 2026)

Malmö University: collaboration with students

The university's collaboration page lists degree projects, project assignments, internships, career events and the Stormathon innovation competition. It does not mention guest lectures [4]. Its alumni page describes mentoring and events, not guest lecturing [11].

What this means: go through course coordinators rather than a central form.

Malmö University: Collaboration with students (checked 16 November 2026)

Course types that tend to use guest lecturers

The examples above point to a few patterns. These are where we would look first.

  1. Master's courses with a practice element. The LUSEM information systems example shows a teacher inviting the authors of the course book [9]. If you have written something used in teaching, mention it.
  2. Creative and cultural industry courses. The K3 master's course builds outside visits into its description [6].
  3. Engineering courses. LTH states that its courses welcome guest lecturers [1].
  4. Entrepreneurship and innovation modules. MaU Innovation suggests speakers to teachers, and VentureLab has run founder talks [5] [10].
  5. Courses close to your daily work. A head of payments fits a finance course better than a general management one. This is a hypothetical example, but the principle is to match the course's learning goals.

Our guide on finding the right course for a guest lecture shows how to read a course page and syllabus.

How to find the right person

Course pages are the most practical starting point. Many name the course coordinator. The page for Music Management & Business, a Lund University course taught at the Malmö Academy of Music, is one example [7]. Course pages also state the language of instruction, which tells you whether to pitch in Swedish or English. That course, for example, is taught in Swedish [7].

If you cannot find a course coordinator, use the central contacts: LTH's External Engagement Coordinator, LUSEM Career Services, or MaU Innovation [1] [2] [5].

What a guest lecture usually looks like

Neither university publishes a standard format. Universities elsewhere give useful reference points. Sheffield University Management School says sessions usually last 30 to 40 minutes plus 15 to 20 minutes of questions [12]. RMIT Europe offers formats from a 15-minute Q&A to a 30-minute presentation, live or pre-recorded [13]. Ask the course coordinator what they need rather than assuming.

When to get in touch

Contact teachers well before the term in which the course runs, while they are still planning it. Adding a session to a course that has already started is harder. Our guide to the academic calendar for guest lecturers explains how Swedish terms and study periods affect timing.

How to pitch a course coordinator

  • Name the course and say why your topic fits its learning outcomes.
  • Offer one clear topic, a length and a format, such as a talk plus Q&A or a short case.
  • Say what students will be able to do or understand afterwards.
  • Make it non-commercial. You are there to teach, not to sell.
  • Keep it short and send one polite follow-up at most.

If a course is not the right fit, student societies often host talks too. See our overview of speaking opportunities in Malmö and Lund.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an alumnus to guest lecture in Lund or Malmö?

No. LTH's guest-lecture page is written for companies and organisations, not alumni only [1]. Being an alumnus can help you reach a teacher, but the course coordinator decides.

Who should I email at Lund University about a guest lecture?

Start with the teacher responsible for the course. If you do not know which course fits, LTH's External Engagement Coordinator or LUSEM Career Services can point you to the right person [1] [2].

Does Malmö University have a guest lecturer sign-up form?

We did not find one. Its collaboration page does not mention guest lectures [4]. Contact course coordinators directly, or MaU Innovation if your topic is innovation or entrepreneurship [5].

Are guest lectures in Malmö and Lund paid?

The pages we checked do not mention fees. Assume a guest lecture is unpaid unless the teacher tells you otherwise.
