Speaking opportunities in Stockholm: universities, student societies and networks

Written by Ted Scheiman, banking and fintech executive, guest lecturer. Last reviewed 17 November 2026. How we research and fact-check

Stockholm has many places that host outside speakers: universities, student associations, international chambers of commerce, industry bodies and tech meetups. The hard part is knowing which ones actually invite people in, and how to reach them.

This page lists only organisations whose own pages show that they host speakers or invite proposals. We opened every page on 16 November 2026. For each one we say what the page shows, the contact route it publishes, and what it does not tell you. We do not rank them. Within each section they appear in alphabetical order.

If you are new to this, start with our guide on how to get speaking opportunities. It covers how to choose a topic and pitch.

Key takeaways

  • Stockholm University and KTH both run alumni routes for guest lectures. KTH also points non-alumni to its student union or to a programme's teacher.
  • Student associations at Stockholm University, KTH and the Stockholm School of Economics (SSE) run panels, lecture series and company sessions with outside guests.
  • The American, British and Dutch chambers in Stockholm run English-language seminars, roundtables and fireside chats.
  • Several tech and product meetups publish an open invitation to speak. AWS User Group Stockholm and DevSum 2027 have formal calls for speakers.
  • None of the pages say whether speakers are paid, except DevSum, which covers travel and accommodation.

How to use this list

Each entry links to the organisation's own page. Contact routes are the ones the organisation publishes: a form, a functional inbox or a contact page. We have not listed anyone's personal email.

Three things to check before you write:

  1. Who the route is for. Some university routes are for alumni only. Read the page before you pitch.
  2. Timing. University teaching runs in terms and periods. Our guide to the academic calendar for guest lectures explains when to send.
  3. Language. Most entries below run English-language programmes or courses. Where a page does not say, we tell you.

Universities and schools

Berghs School of Communication

Berghs states that it brings in guest lecturers and professionals from around the world. Its article on international guest speakers, published in March 2025, ends: "Passing through Sweden and interested in delivering a guest lecture to Berghs students? Don't hesitate to reach out!" [7]

  • Fits: communication, marketing, design and creative work.
  • Route: the "Contact us" link on the Berghs guest speaker article. The page also lists the switchboard, +46 8-587 550 00.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

KTH Royal Institute of Technology

KTH Alumni runs a register for guest lecturing. The page says that, together with KTH Career Service, it organises guest lectures where alumni share their professional experience and career journeys. Questions go to alumni@kth.se [2].

For non-alumni, KTH's collaboration page says you can contact the Student Union at naringsliv@ths.kth.se, or contact the teacher of a specific programme. It describes guest lecturing as lecturing on a specific subject while representing your industry [3]. KTH offers 60 master's programmes in English [25].

  • Fits: engineering, technology, AI, industry practice.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Stockholm School of Economics: SSE Tech Initiative

The SSE Tech Initiative "organizes talks with entrepreneurs, executives, and thought leaders", alongside seminars and workshops [4]. Its list of past tech talks includes speakers from Spotify, Google, Mastercard, Tink, Microsoft Sweden, IBM Sweden and Scania [6].

The current series "Technology in an uncertain world" covers AI, startups, cybersecurity and security technology. Its page says: "we would be very happy to hear from you" to anyone who wants to contribute in future, and gives techinitiative@hhs.se [5]. The most recent event listed was a tech talk on 12 November 2026.

  • Fits: technology and business, startups, AI, security.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Stockholm University

The Stockholm University alumni page says guest lecturers who can share real-world experience are "in high demand", by students and by course leaders at many departments. Alumni are asked to email alumni@su.se with an idea for a talk, and the university will "do our best to match you with the right student audience" [1]. Stockholm University offers more than 100 master's programmes taught in English [26].

  • Fits: any field taught at the university, matched by the alumni office.
  • Route: alumni@su.se (for alumni).
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

For how these institutions use guest lecturers in courses, see guest lecturing in Stockholm. For the general process, read how to become a guest lecturer at a university.

Student associations and societies

Student societies often book speakers for evening panels, lunch sessions and company events. Our guide to speaking at student societies explains how their boards work and when they plan.

Entrepreneurship Society, SASSE (Stockholm School of Economics)

The Entrepreneurship Society is part of the Student Association at SSE. It runs Initiate, "a series of inspirational lectures by entrepreneurs to inspire SSE students to entrepreneurship". Previous Initiate speakers listed on the page include the founders of iZettle, Karma, Sana Labs and Eytys [12].

  • Fits: founders and people with startup experience.
  • Route: the board inboxes listed on the society's page.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Föreningen Ekonomerna (F.E.St.) Finance Society, Stockholm University

Föreningen Ekonomerna is the Business Association at Stockholm University and publishes info@fest.se [9]. Its Finance Society says it invites employers to campus for "interactive sessions, panel discussions, and networking events" and arranges company visits [8].

  • Fits: finance, fintech and finance careers. The page frames sessions around employers, so a pitch tied to your organisation may land better than a solo pitch.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

KTH AI Society

The KTH AI Society runs educational events from introductory machine learning courses to research-level topics, plus hackathons and workshops with companies. Its contact is contact@kthais.com [10]. It has hosted an in-person breakfast panel, "Building Business with AI", with Kinnevik and Tandem Health [11].

  • Fits: AI in business, investment, founders building with AI.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

SASSE Tech Committee (Stockholm School of Economics)

An SSE news article from 2026 describes the student-led Tech Committee, which runs Tech Days, a fintech group and an AI Society. Tech Days in May 2026 included lunch sessions with early-stage tech founders and a serial entrepreneur [13]. SASSE publishes reception@sasse.se on its event pages [27].

  • Fits: fintech, AI, tech careers.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Chambers of commerce and industry associations

Chambers and associations put you in front of working professionals rather than students. Our guide to speaking at industry associations and chambers covers how they choose speakers.

American Chamber of Commerce in Sweden (AmCham Sweden)

AmCham Sweden's programme runs in English and includes breakfast seminars, Executive Forum roundtables, panel discussions, fireside chats and webinars. Upcoming items include "Fireside Chat: Turn Intelligence into your Competitive Advantage" on 19 January 2027. Events are aimed mainly at members and invited guests, and some are invitation-only [14].

  • Route: the chamber's contact page.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

British-Swedish Chamber of Commerce (BSCC)

The BSCC events page lists morning briefings, seminars, roundtables and fireside chats, often hosted at member firms. Upcoming events include "Fireside Chat: AI and The Future of Enterprise" on 19 January 2027 and "Redefining Leadership 2027" on 12 March 2027, with speakers to be announced. The secretariat inbox is secretariat@bscc.info [15].

  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Dutch Chamber of Commerce in Sweden

The Dutch Chamber hosted an interactive Early Breakfast seminar on AI in May 2025, with two external speakers from industry discussing what large language models can and cannot do for productivity [16].

  • Route: the chamber's contact page, linked from its site.
  • Checked 16 November 2026. The page does not show a fixed seminar schedule.

Nordic Blockchain Association

The Nordic Blockchain Association describes itself as the leading industry organisation for blockchain, web3 and crypto stakeholders in the Nordics and Baltics. Its event calendar lists the 8th Nordic Blockchain Conference, 26 to 27 May in Stockholm, and online talks such as "What Nordic Banks Are Actually Doing with Blockchain" [17].

  • Fits: crypto, digital assets, payments.
  • Checked 16 November 2026. The calendar page does not state the conference year or publish a speaker call.

Meetups and conferences

Meetups are often the easiest first slot. See our guide to getting a first meetup talk.

AWS User Group Stockholm

Its call for speakers is open from 5 November 2026 to 3 November 2027. It welcomes talks at any level about lessons learned, AWS services, projects and demos, and states that sales speeches are not accepted. Placeholder meetup dates are 16 February, 23 March and 20 April 2027 [18].

  • Checked 16 November 2026.

DevSum 2027

DevSum 2027 runs on 27 to 28 May 2027 at Södra Teatern, with workshops on 26 May. All sessions are in English. The call for speakers closes on 8 December 2026. Speakers get travel and accommodation covered and the event fee waived [24].

  • Fits: software development, cloud, AI and agents, people and culture.
  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Product People breakfast panels

Product People runs morning breakfast panels in Stockholm for "20-30 local product professionals" on AI and product work. The event page invites people to "be on the panel or introduce us to great speakers" via offline-events@getproductpeople.com [22].

  • Checked 16 November 2026. The page we checked was a past event.

ProductTank Stockholm

ProductTank is Mind the Product's local meetup format. The June 2026 ProductTank Stockholm event had three talks by product managers and a fireside panel. It asked: "Are you interested in speaking at the next ProductTank event?" and pointed to the organisers on Meetup [21]. Mind the Product's speaker page suggests new speakers start at a local ProductTank [20].

  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Stockholm AI, ML & Data Developers Group

This Meetup group has about 1,345 members and runs tech talks, study jams and code labs. Its page says: "If you'd like to speak at future meetups", reach out to the organisers. Its listed events are online [23].

  • Checked 16 November 2026.

Stockholm MLOps Community

The Stockholm MLOps Community has about 5,868 members and runs frequent evening meetups, with events listed for 19 November, 3 December and 10 December 2026 [19].

  • Checked 16 November 2026. The page does not publish an open call. Speakers appear to be invited, so contact the organisers through Meetup with a specific talk idea.

Incubators and startup hubs

Incubators run workshops, coaching and demo days for founders. For an expert in funding, law, sales, product or hiring, they can be a practical audience. The hub pages we checked do not publish an open call for speakers, so pitch a specific session that helps early-stage teams.

Sting

Sting is a Stockholm organisation that supports early-stage startups. Its site invites people to "join our in-person and online events", and lists a Demo Day, a workshop series for new teams called Sting Test Drive, and 20-minute Open Coaching sessions with its startup coaches [28].

  • Fits: funding, go-to-market, product, legal and hiring topics for early teams.
  • Checked 17 November 2026. The page does not say how outside speakers are chosen. Use the contact route on the site with a concrete workshop idea.

KTH Innovation

KTH Innovation keeps an events calendar on KTH's website. Its page for KTH Innovation Collide, an evening held in September 2022, describes "inspirational meetings with founders, demos from startups, and matchmaking". The calendar also listed the KTH Innovation Readiness Level Summit 2026 [29].

  • Fits: innovation, deep tech, commercialising research.
  • Checked 17 November 2026. The Collide page is from 2022. Check the calendar for current events before you pitch.

Left out: we also looked for SUP46 (Start-up People of Sweden), which hosted startup events at Regeringsgatan in central Stockholm. The only event page we could open was from 2015 [30], so we could not confirm what it runs today.

For other cities, see speaking opportunities in Uppsala and our overview of speaking opportunities in Sweden, city by city.

What we could not confirm

  • Pay: apart from DevSum, no page states whether speakers are paid or receive expenses. Ask before you accept.
  • Selection criteria: most pages do not say how speakers are chosen.
  • Dates: student societies rarely publish a schedule in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be an alumnus to guest lecture at a Stockholm university?

The Stockholm University and KTH routes listed here are alumni routes. KTH also tells non-alumni to contact the Student Union or a programme's teacher. Berghs invites international guest lecturers in general.

Can I give a talk in English in Stockholm?

Yes, in many places. AmCham Sweden's programme is in English, DevSum states that all sessions are in English, and Stockholm University and KTH run many master's programmes in English. Check each organiser's page for the event you target.

Will I be paid to speak?

Usually the pages do not say. DevSum 2027 covers speaker travel and accommodation and waives the event fee. For everything else, ask the organiser before you accept.

Which is the easiest first step?

Open calls are the most direct: AWS User Group Stockholm and DevSum 2027 both have one. Meetups such as ProductTank Stockholm and the Stockholm AI, ML & Data Developers Group also invite people to get in touch about speaking.
