Background

Ted Scheiman has more than 25 years of experience in banking, payments, consulting and investing, much of it in London, Frankfurt and Singapore. He is country manager for Sweden at Firi, the Nordic crypto exchange, chairs the board of the fintech company OneMoneyWay, sits on the board of Sweden Blockchain and invests as an angel in early-stage companies.

What Ted writes about

  • Articles on finding and getting speaking opportunities: guest lectures, conferences, panels, meetups, associations and podcasts.
  • Advice on contacting organisers, preparing a talk and building a reputation through speaking.
  • City and topic guides to speaking opportunities in Sweden.

Every article is researched against primary sources, such as university, organiser and event pages, and Ted checks that the advice matches how organisers and universities actually work before it is published. Articles are updated when sources change.

Independence

Ted has a financial interest in Spoke At. No organiser, course provider or event pays to be listed or recommended in his articles.
